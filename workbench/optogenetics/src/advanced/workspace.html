Release verification · 100 tests / 65 browser assertions

100 automated numerical/import tests passed, including all 46 retained core tests. Three real Chromium flows passed 65 assertions covering calculations, replay, cancellation, imports, exports, desktop and 390-pixel mobile layout. Physical iPhone and Safari have not been tested.

The reduced photocycle implementation matches 48 independent SciPy matrix-exponential cases to about 1 × 10⁻¹⁴ in state probability. Nine independent chloride integrations agree to about 6 × 10⁻¹⁴ mM. These checks verify the chosen equations, not biological calibration.

The included short inhibition refinement (0.01 → 0.005 ms) changed somatic voltage by at most 0.0024 mV. The inherited morphology-refinement limit remains. Thermal hotspots are unresolved: the microLED example peaks at 0.557 °C on 80 µm voxels and 1.012 °C on 48 µm voxels. No safety threshold is asserted.
