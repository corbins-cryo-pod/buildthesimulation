# Finger Pathway Atlas V4

V4 integrates the original V3 detailed cellular models and anatomical surfaces into one deterministic world-registered cell graph. V3 and V4 have independent public pages at `/simulations/finger-pathway-atlas/v3/` and `/simulations/finger-pathway-atlas/v4/`, with separate simulation listings, version navigation and shared reference plates. The existing `/simulations/finger-pathway-atlas/` route remains a V4 alias. The complete original V3 viewer and its untouched source assets remain under `v3-viewer` and `assets/finger-pathway-atlas/v3`; V4 uses `/simulations/finger-pathway-atlas/viewer/`.

## Model

- 306 persistent cell identities in 78 modeled routes: 52 tactile, eight free-ending nociceptive, six proprioceptive, and 12 motor-unit examples.
- Primary sensory neurons have a soma-to-T-junction stem and a continuous peripheral/central axon. DRG is not a synaptic relay.
- Touch and conscious proprioception relay in ipsilateral cuneate, cross in medulla, relay in contralateral VPL and terminate at separate cortical receivers with simplified overview rendering.
- Nociceptive examples relay in the dorsal horn and cross in spinal cord before ascending through brainstem to thalamus and cortex.
- Motor routes use separate M1, spinal-interneuron and lower-motor identities, cross caudally in medulla, leave ventrally and bypass DRG. Median and radial muscle endpoints were corrected against retained source muscle mesh neighborhoods. Ulnar intrinsic-muscle placement remains qualitative.
- Original V3 DRG membranes and satellite patches, spinal/brainstem/thalamic arbors, cutaneous endings, NMJ components and all ten measured human cortical trace templates are retained at exactly 0.001 mm per micrometre. Each cell has a deterministic source-template registration. Original axon/stem ports join the continuous V4 trajectories. SA-II and group-II spindle endings, which lack corresponding V3 templates, retain separately labeled modeled details.
- Source mesh vertices, normals, indices, colors and original captured centerlines are preserved. Two support envelopes close the old Pacinian and spindle inspection openings while preserving their lamellar/capsule complexity. Tissue blocks, generic section planes, window rims and disconnected context fragments remain excluded outside the dedicated M1/S1 cortex view.
- Cortical traces retain their measured source IDs, layer and region. Their S1/M1 placement and long-range connection are illustrative, not measured cortical identities. V3 optional modeled spines/boutons remain available at High detail.

## Paired M1 and S1 cortex

The cortical endpoint is now limited to the two gyri needed by this pathway rather than displaying the full collection of generic cortical scaffold surfaces:

- **M1 / precentral gyrus:** one coronal-orientation cut face with six explicit layers. The layer bookkeeping retains I–VI, but the M1 prior is agranular: layer IV is very thin/indistinct, layers III and V are expanded, and the largest representative corticofugal somata occupy layer V.
- **S1 / postcentral gyrus:** one separate coronal-orientation cut face with six explicit layers. S1 uses a granular prior with an expanded thalamorecipient layer IV.
- The two cut faces remain visible together in the same cortex zoom. The gross gyri remain at the cortical endpoint in the body-scale world; the paired layer faces, white-matter boundaries and cellular detail are distance-based LODs, not replacement scenes.
- Each section includes a one-millimetre H01-scale inspection window, deterministic representative somata, dendrite/axon fragments, glia, microvessels and representative synaptic puncta. These objects reproduce an H01-inspired visual and morphological vocabulary; they are not copied H01 segment IDs or measured M1/S1 synapses.
- H01 is temporal cortex. M1/S1 layer proportions and motor-versus-sensory differences therefore come from BigBrain and cytoarchitectonic literature rather than transferring the H01 temporal laminar profile verbatim.
- DKI-informed orientation envelopes show an M1 corticofugal/corticospinal family, an S1 thalamocortical family and short central-sulcus association fibers. Multiple nearby streamlines encode orientation uncertainty. DKI is not used to assert individual axon identity or exact endpoints.
- The coronal MRI-style plane is an orientation/registration context generated by the model. It is not a patient scan or copied atlas image.

The machine-readable scientific boundary, selected layer priors and primary references are stored in `public/assets/finger-pathway-atlas/v4/cortex-sources.json`.

## Rendering and interaction

All anatomy, somata, terminal fields and routes use the same millimeter coordinate system. Full cell recipes and low-detail paths exist independently of visibility. Camera navigation does not exchange microscopic scenes or multiply cell dimensions.

Initial anatomy is a 1,030,739-byte gzip scaffold containing all 86 original anatomical surfaces (41 bones, four muscles, 35 cortical surfaces, five CNS structures and skin). The legacy generic cortical surfaces remain in the retained scaffold/provenance package but are suppressed at render time; the paired M1/S1 volume is the only visible cortex in V4. Near the camera, noncortical individual surfaces swap to exact original V3 position/index arrays: 523,602 vertices / 1,053,311 triangles in total. These original surfaces are loaded individually, not at startup.

The cell manifest preserves 159 original source templates. Cellular meshes are compressed individually and loaded on demand; retaining the complete original geometry requires about 67 MB of compressed assets in total, but the entire collection is not required at startup. The largest individual source template is about 4 MB. Decoded cellular templates have a 64 MB / 48-template cache. Rendered cellular details are bounded by source triangle cost and 4/8/14 objects, with a 24-entry / 1.8-million-triangle cache. An inspected original model is never decimated to fit the object budget. Whole-anatomy originals have a separate bounded cache. Original arbors replace provisional local trees; there are no duplicate procedural arbors under the restored mesh.

The paired cortex uses three distance bands: gross gyri, six-layer cut faces, then H01-inspired cellular sampling for both sections together. Instancing is used for somata and glia, while tract families are bounded tube bundles. Cellular symbol sizes have a small readability floor and are not stereological density estimates.

Rendering stops while idle or tab-hidden. Source loading is coalesced, timed out and retryable. Delayed inspection requests cannot override a later selection or manual navigation. Pixel ratio is bounded by selected quality. Mesh vertices use local source anchors and rigid transforms to preserve micrometre features at body-scale coordinates.

Region navigation, route families, individual cell selection, route isolation, whole-path highlighting, cell/ending focus, export of complete cell registrations and source manifests (already-loaded centerlines are explicitly labeled), scale bar, source notes, fullscreen and a collapsible inspector are included. Focus uses actual intact object bounds and has portrait/landscape framing regressions. The only cut surfaces are the explicitly labeled paired M1/S1 coronal cortex sections.

## Scientific scope

This is a representative anatomical construction, not all biological neurons in a finger, an experimentally measured connectome, or a solver-ready membrane model. Source figures span tissues/species and independently magnified panels. Synapses are explicit modeled graph attachments, not mapped subcellular contacts. The paired M1/S1 cortex is a multiscale modeled specimen: H01 supplies ultrastructural vocabulary, BigBrain supplies six-layer regional priors, and DKI supplies probabilistic orientation constraints. None of those sources supplies a matched end-to-end finger connectome. Cerebellar, autonomic, reticular and other collateral networks are outside this selected circuit. The supplied gross skin preserves its earlier upper-body crop.

The source comparison plates still show V3 on their left panels and are labeled accordingly. Original scientific figure files and attribution remain unchanged. See `public/assets/finger-pathway-atlas/v4/provenance.json`, `scaffold-provenance.json`, and `cortex-sources.json` for source records and derivation.

## Verification

`npm run build` includes model/morphology/focus regression tests and the existing repository tests. `node scripts/verify-finger-atlas-v4.mjs --build` checks published routes, asset presence, geometry/index validity, gzip identity, source hashes and startup payload. The original V3 geometry/reference verification remains in CI.

The M1/S1 module is syntax-checked independently and its source record is JSON-validated before publication. Preservation tests continue to compare original V3 geometry bytes, material values, source paths, source-template coverage, world-port continuity and actual object framing. Offline inspection projects actual restored source anatomy and cell mesh triangles. This checks geometry appearance, not browser lighting or interaction. Interactive WebGL QA remains dependent on a browser-capable environment; no browser frame-rate claim is made without one.
