What is actually simulated?

This is an independent, exploratory prototype. It explores categories of neuronal remodeling behavior, not a fitted reconstruction of a particular cell or an estimate of molecular rates. Micrometres and minutes are nominal model units.

Numerical state

The soma has 96 deformable radial boundary nodes and local activator/inhibitor fields. Each neurite has finite-radius membrane geometry, an extensible chain of overdamped mechanical nodes, yielding substrate anchors, local growth-cone activity, a structural-support variable, a deformable 48-node fan, and independent transient filopodia. Lateral processes may stabilize or withdraw. The soma boundary is star-convex: it cannot represent membrane holes, arbitrary folds, or migration topology.

Coupling, rather than identical growth

A phenomenological FitzHugh–Nagumo-type relaxation oscillator drives a global contractile episode and sends a wave into a selected process. Wave arrival temporarily relaxes local contractility and boosts local activity. This is a reduced mechanism inspired by the cited experiments, not a molecular reconstruction. The default transport speed is 2.2 µm/min, within the 2–3 µm/min average speed reported in Winans et al. (2016); other coefficients are exploratory.

du/dt = 0.86 (u − u³/3 − v + 0.64) dv/dt = 0.86 × 0.085 (u + 0.7 − 0.8v) Tip material rate = protrusive drive − retractile drive Node motion = overdamped elastic + bending + adhesion forces Cortical radius rate = protrusion − contraction − restoring force + neighbor smoothing + area penalty Wave position rate = selected transport speed Structural support = activity-dependent maturation − turnover

Local growth bursts are stochastic switches, not a movie loop. A common neurite-length budget limits added process material and recycles withdrawals. It is an effective resource constraint, not conservation of full cell volume, actin, membrane lipids, or molecular mass. Deformed arclength is reported separately from added material.

What the image means

Green displays a synthetic structural-scaffold channel. Magenta displays synthetic cortical/protrusive activity. The membrane mask is a union of the soma, flared shafts, growth-cone fans, and fine protrusions. A shader adds optical texture and a modest blur. Individual rendered fibers and puncta are illustrative texture, not simulated microtubules or identified organelles. The nucleus is an image inclusion, not a mechanically solved nucleus.

Projection crossings do not create a synapse or merge process graphs. There is no steric collision solver, out-of-plane dynamics, receptor kinetics, electrophysiology, or calibrated microscope point-spread function.

Reproducibility and numerical limits

The solver uses a fixed 0.04-minute step. The playback selector changes how many solver steps run, not their size. A seed reproduces an unperturbed run; saved states also preserve the random generator. Manual interventions affect the trajectory. Rendering never consumes the solver's randomness. To prevent a hidden-tab runaway, paused/hidden browser time is not simulated. On a slow machine playback may fall behind; no larger numerical step is substituted.

Bounds: 14 concurrent processes, 140 µm growth cap per process, finite soma/fan radii, 2,400 recent one-minute measurement samples, and 500 recent log entries. Exports report the retained measurement window. These are prototype constraints, not biological constants.

Primary scientific references

An intrinsic cytoskeletal oscillator establishes neuronal polarity. Nature (2026). Basis for testing soma-driven contraction followed by selective wave delivery; not a universal rule for all neuron types or developmental stages.

Winans, Collins & Meyer. Waves of actin and microtubule polymerization drive microtubule-based transport and neurite growth before single axon formation. eLife (2016). Wave transport and transient widening motivate a reduced wave state.

Stochastic Actin Polymerization and Steady Retrograde Flow Determine Growth Cone Advancement. Biophysical Journal (2009). Local advancement is represented as a competition, not a scripted extension speed.

Actin Waves Do Not Boost Neurite Outgrowth in the Early Stages of Neuron Maturation (2017). A caution against equating wave arrival with guaranteed net growth; the prototype allows cycles without net extension.

Software verification checks numerical invariants and interface behavior. It does not establish biological validity. No experimental image tracking, quantitative fitting, or held-out predictive test has been performed.
