## Source and geometry review

Each added image is stored locally and records its original source URL, asset URL, source dimensions, SHA-256, changes, credit, hardware-version note and rights statement. Sources include primary papers, author manuscripts, manufacturer manuals, original institutional releases in Mandarin and English, a Danish IFESS presentation, the Science Museum Group, and an official Dutch repository. No authenticated or nonpublic files were available for this collection. Third-party access blocks were not bypassed.

Photographs, optical micrographs, electron micrographs and mixed source figures are distinguished. Schematics and product renders are explicitly identified in captions; they do not establish physical construction. Complete no-derivatives figures remain intact. Copyright-retained source references are labeled as owner-requested noncommercial references; no Creative Commons grant or manufacturer permission is asserted for them.

Visible scale bars and dimension annotations are preserved. Fingers, coins, surgical images, perspective views and renders are not treated as calibrated rulers. Images alone do not establish device thickness, hidden structures, tolerances, material layers, contact impedance, or electrical performance. Source-specific missing views and geometric limits remain in captions and the per-record research registry.

The exact Neuropixels Ultra figure already appears in the Neuropixels family gallery, so the Ultra sheet links there. Existing shared-hardware references remain canonical. The card selector can choose the strongest existing device view independently of gallery order; this improves Neuropixels 1.0, the SIMIT mosquito-inspired probe assembly and the integrated-microwire microchannel interface while preserving the owner's gallery sequence.

The full-resolution link in the image dialog opens the local original for contact and scale-bar inspection. Scale and geometry notes appear when recorded.
