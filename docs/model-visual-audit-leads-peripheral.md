# Lead and peripheral visual audit — 2026-10-15

Scope: all eight lead-window models and all four peripheral models. Source images were opened and visually inspected, in addition to rereading the dimensions and materials. The eight lead windows retain their existing contact centers, diameters and lengths. Shading is illustrative; a color or gloss setting is not a measured optical property.

## Geometry and presentation recommendations

- Round the FINE lumen in the XY cross-section while leaving the planar electrode-bearing top/bottom regions and all contact positions intact. Its contact x extents stop at ±3.75 mm, inside the ±4.25 mm planar region of the proposed capsule lumen.
- Keep geometry units and physical diameters unchanged. Do not enlarge lead rings for visibility or shorten the LIFE contact to make its aspect ratio less extreme.
- Cuff contacts point inward. Show enough translucency, or use an explicit contacts-only detail view, so the first useful view does not hide the neural interface behind the sleeve.
- Avoid arbitrary decorative wires, surface texturing, grooves and end caps. Source-supported material contrast and rounded FINE silicone are the changes justified by this comparison.
