## Primary source record

- [Steinmetz et al., Neuropixels 2.0 (2021), Figure 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8244810/).
- [imec Neuropixels 2.0 datasheet](https://www.neuropixels.org/_files/ugd/328966_2b39661f072d405b8d284c3c73588bc6.pdf): material specification, shank, contact pitch and tip dimensions.
- [imec Quad Base datasheet](https://www.neuropixels.org/_files/ugd/328966_4e39ab2e46424dc9b3efa446d286ab0f.pdf): Figure 1 annotated shank SEM, Figure 2 porous TiN electrode, dimensions table.
- [Neuropixels Central technology](https://www.neuropixelscentral.org/technology): NXT's stated identity with NP2 shank geometry and site layout.
- [imec NP1032 NHP datasheet](https://www.neuropixels.org/_files/ugd/328966_4228a68261964794ad55f98d80ac3f45.pdf): p. 2 Figure 2 distinguishes the long/medium shank; p. 3 specifies 342 µm tip and approximately 25° out-of-plane sharpening.
- [imec Neuropixels 1.0 datasheet](https://www.neuropixels.org/_files/ugd/832f20_4a14406ba1204e60ae8534b09e201b49.pdf): p. 3 specifies 175 µm chisel, porous TiN, silicon-nitride front, silicon-dioxide back and silicon sidewall.
- [Patel et al. (2015)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4789140/): Methods 2.1.1–2.1.2 and chronic-array Results 3.2, supporting pitch, coating/core, cut-face treatment and nominal length.

The existing 175 versus 206 µm NP2 and 342 versus 373 µm NHP taper discrepancies between manufacturer PDFs and ProbeTable remain disclosed. A sharper rendering must not silently select a different hardware revision. SEM grayscale is not a calibrated visible-light material measurement. The ideal planar bevel and optical appearance do not provide insertion-mechanics or electrochemical fidelity.
