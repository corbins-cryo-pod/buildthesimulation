# Additional cortical-surface model evidence

Reviewed 2026-10-15. These entries extend `src/lib/devices/models-surface.js`.
Dimensions are in millimetres internally. Regular contact coordinates are
reconstructions, not released fabrication masks or acquisition channel maps.
Every model declares its scope and separates documented dimensions from
reconstructed or omitted geometry.

| Device ID | Named geometry | Evidence and deliberate limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BTSD-ACAD-0008 | Viventi 2011, 360 contacts | [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3235709/), Fig. 1 and Methods: 18 × 20, 500 µm pitch, 300 µm squares, approximately 25 µm assembled film. 10 × 9 mm is a recording crop; not full outline. |
| BTSD-ACAD-0090 | SIMIT 2025, 256 contacts | [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12677598/), fabrication section: 16 × 16, 850 µm circles, 1,250 µm pitch. Stack sum is nominal, based on 7 µm PI, 5 nm Cr, 150 nm Au, 13 µm PI. Mesh/perfusion holes and thickened lead regions are excluded. |
| BTSD-ACAD-0099 | CIBR aGel, underlying 1,024-contact patch | [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12713035/), section 2.3 and Fig. 3b: 32 × 32, 20 µm contacts, 200 µm pitch, 6 µm PI. The figure labels 6.2 mm center span. Crop is 6.4 mm. The important 10 µm hydrogel is explicitly absent; the model must not be used to infer tissue-to-metal separation of the coated device. |
| BTSD-ACAD-0048 | UCSD 2024 transparent graphene, 256-contact patch | [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11742260/), Fig. 1e and Methods: 20 µm openings, 350 µm pitch, 14 µm + 2 µm parylene. 16 × 16 layout reconstructed from square array image; 5.6 mm crop is not the clear imaging area's outline. |
| BTSD-ACAD-0047 | CLEAR rat-size 2016 protocol, 16 contacts | [Primary protocol](https://www.nature.com/articles/nprot.2016.127), device design: rat 200 µm contacts / 750 µm pitch / 25 µm parylene. Named revision avoids assigning the mouse 150 µm / 500 µm design to the rat array. The 3 mm crop is not the full film. |
| BTSD-ACAD-0042 | PMT speech grid 2021, 128 contacts | [Primary inventor disclosure](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240366157A1/en), Example 1: 16 × 8, 4 mm pitch, 67 × 35 × 0.51 mm, 2 mm discs. Exposed area is not separately established: `contactAreaKnown: false` must produce null area on export. Centering and flush discs are reconstruction. Does not represent the 2023 253-site configuration. |
| BTSD-ACAD-0050 | BISC 2025, dense 1,024-contact crop | [Published primary manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13327737/), implant form factor: 14 µm TiN squares, 26.5 × 29 µm pitch. 32 × 32 crop avoids claiming full 65,536-site chip geometry. Thickness 50 µm is a display upper bound; full device is reported below 50 µm. |

The source references and in-view model notes carry the configuration and
scope. Rectangular crop boundaries, flat poses, schematic contact-well walls,
and nominal stack sums do not imply manufacturing tolerances or a recovered
implant silhouette. Coating relief, trace routing, encapsulation mechanics,
cortical deformation, field spread and electrical material models are outside
these geometric references.

Deferred after review: Precision Layer 7 (mixed recording/stimulation/reference
map and assembly thickness), WIMAGINE (curved cranial housing and references
need a dedicated mesh), e-dura (configuration-specific contact map and chemical
channels), NeuroXess/Triple-F (variant-specific outline and stack), NeuroCam
(contact dimensions/stack), CHIP hydrogel (complete layout), Fudan MNIS
(rectangular capacitive transistor nodes need a different contact primitive),
Flex2Chip in-vivo patch (exact implanted contact map), and SIAT IntE-LIG
(missing dimensions). No additional models were generated by borrowing a
nearby device's thickness, contact size or pitch.
