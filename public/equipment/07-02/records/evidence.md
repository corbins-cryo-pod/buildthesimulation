**Replay.** Each CSV is an independent plotted run, elapsed time in seconds from its own graph origin. Do not superimpose them as a jointly observed set, add constant channels and call them observed, or combine into a single measured experiment. Use 07-02 TC CO₂ as default; temperature is available as a separate replay if desired. The channels correspond to the blue traces; the red horizontal rules are set points. Piecewise-linear interpolation between selected digitized points is a reconstruction. No raw data or authorization to redistribute manufacturer artwork is asserted. Link out to the original figures.

**Calibration and precision.** Coordinates in original embedded rasters, zero indexed from top left. TC p. 5 (959×693 px): x=78..895 is 0..30 min; y=618..68 is 0..6 vol %. Temperature p. 2 (958×691 px): x=85..863 is 0..30 min; y=614..64 is 28..38 °C. Read the center of the blue trace at selected ordinates through the steep limbs and at abscissas across shallower segments. Where the red setpoint overlaps the blue trace, approximate coincident readings by the setpoint within the line width. One horizontal pixel ≈2.2–2.3 s, one vertical pixel ≈0.011 percentage points or 0.018 °C; a stroke spans about five pixels. Reported tolerances cover reading/location of the plotted extrema; they are not sensor uncertainties or error bounds for interpolation at arbitrary fixed times during a steep fall.

**Acceptance example.** TC sample A occupies shelf 1 from 0 to 600 s and sample B shelf 3 from 900 to 1200 s. A receives the trough, B does not, and B's minimum rounds to 5.00% over its scheduled interval. A 30 s interval of illustrative opening may be aligned at [255,285) for the TC trace or [326,356) for temperature, inferred from onset of departure. The graph confirms an opening of 30 s but absence of recorded switch edges means the modeled open/close times should be labeled inferred. No inference that the minimum should coincide with door closure is needed. Alternative acceptance without inferred event edges uses the directly reconstructable depressed-concentration span (<4.5 vol %), observed only by A. Recovery of the chamber continues after door closure. The figures print recovery times of 5.5 min TC and 5.2 min temperature; this PDF omits the manufacturer's exact timing origin and criterion, so do not treat them as exponential time constants.
