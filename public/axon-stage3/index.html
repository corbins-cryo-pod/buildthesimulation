The model boundary

This is a new exploratory growth module for the B3 complete-ganglion-replacement hypothesis. Fixed soma positions acquire two growing processes. We compute synthetic growth trajectories and bookkeeping; we do not claim that these are measured human spiral ganglion morphologies or that a target zone is a functioning organ connection.

The original Stage 1 electrical model and its numerical evidence are unchanged. No Stage 1 verification badge is inherited by this engine. Stage 2 remains unfinished. This module is a development preview, not an approved sequential Stage 3 release.

Growth and boundaries

Each process switches independently among extension, pause and retraction. Event waiting times are exponentially distributed. Branch attempts use an independent Poisson clock, with an explicit per-cell branch ceiling. Extension uses persistent direction, scaffold alignment, nearest-lane haptotaxis, target-field chemotaxis and angular diffusion. All targets contribute to the cue field; the correct target label is not used to steer.

Sidewalls, annular endcaps and outer domain walls use swept line–surface intersections and specular reflection. Rendered paths include reflection points. Branchpoints anchor their parent paths during retraction. Neurites can cross each other; finite neurite diameter, soma motility, cytoskeletal forces, and adhesive mechanics are not solved. The repair-support and debris fields are phenomenological proxies motivated by Schwann-cell biology.

Identity and integration

Empty and multiply occupied wells are recorded explicitly in Poisson-loading mode; only exactly one-cell wells enter growth. The alternative assumes successful one-cell placement and is not a measured loading yield. Targets are abstract geometric zones with assigned labels. Sustained encounters become candidates; a separate opt-in transmission probability can label them “assumed-functional.” It never demonstrates conduction, a synapse, or stimulation selectivity.

The matched no-device comparator is a structural B3 ledger only: original identities are absent in both replacement conditions, and no donor neurons are introduced in the no-device case. It is not a simulation of hollow-conduit repair. The handoff requires confirmation and exports a pending identity manifest. Its electrical geometry and recruitment fields are deliberately null.

Verification status

Verification report loading. No biological validation is claimed.

Analytic diffusion, independent dense linear algebra, numerical quadrature, and an independent continuous-ODE reference test component equations. Contract tests check determinism, separate random streams, geometry, loading, contact state, malformed settings, budgets, cancellation and export integrity. These tests do not establish whole-model stochastic convergence or a calibrated growth-to-function relationship.
