Neural Growth V5 / Model card

A unified, testable growth laboratory.

Deformable cells, branching neurites, transported growth material, local activity and tissue fields share one saved state. Ten experiments change the preparation and boundary conditions of that engine.

What the model calculates

A triangulated elastic soma couples to persistent, deformable neurite shafts. Growth cones explore locally; adhesion, cytoskeletal drive, membrane reserve and delivered material limit extension. Uncommitted processes compete for polarity. Contacts must persist and mature before the reduced network assay can transmit through them.

What each view means

Phase and fluorescence are illustrative display functions, not calibrated optical images. The structural projection displays the same graph and membrane mesh. Activity colors show a phenomenological activator/recovery system or an optional traveling-packet hypothesis. Cargo dots show modeled transport parcels, not resolved vesicles.

Evidence and assumptions

Hippocampal culture motivates early polarity (Dotti et al., 1988). Actin-associated transport motivates an optional growth hypothesis (Winans et al., 2016); activity is not treated as a universal cause of outgrowth. The 3D profile includes a reduced adhesion-independent drive inspired by Santos et al., 2020. Structural contact is separated from functional transmission, motivated by Christopherson et al., 2005.

The gel cue is a scalar diffusion–decay field in relative units. Repair support agents move by an explicit effective rule across a 300 µm model gap. This is not a fitted Schwann-cell, vascular, immune or clinical nerve-regeneration model. Electrode surfaces are inert obstacles; electrical stimulation fields are not calculated.

Electrical scope

The passive cable uses the actual grown tree with illustrative Cₘ = 1 µF/cm², gₗ = 0.1 mS/cm² and Rₐ = 150 Ω·cm. It has no active channels. A separate point-neuron network assay compares transmission enabled versus blocked. Neither assay calibrates the grown cells to recorded electrophysiology or establishes functional recovery.

Reproducibility and limits

Growth uses seeded random streams and explicit units. Full-state import validates topology and material ledgers. Diffusion substeps respect a positivity bound. Numeric and software checks do not constitute biological validation. Spatial resolution, stochastic timesteps, arbitrary rate coefficients and finite budgets can affect results.
