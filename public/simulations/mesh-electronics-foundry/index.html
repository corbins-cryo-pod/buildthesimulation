    REFERENCES & SCOPE

    Where the numbers came from

    Directly reported

    Separate SU-8, Cr/Au, Cr/Pt, and top SU-8 masks on a nickel release layer. The 32-site reference describes 20 µm Pt disks, a roughly 2.2 × 38 mm specimen, and two Au leads per longitudinal rail. The connector pitch is 500 µm; pads are 400 µm wide against approximately 100 µm pins.

    At 1 kHz, the authors reported a mean 1.3 MΩ and −80° for 32 disks in 1× PBS. A separate contact-resistance measurement was approximately 3 Ω per interface. These are two different experiments.

    Modeled, not recovered

    Diagram artwork, E→A→P order, nominal structure dimensions where unspecified, sheet resistance, solver contact area, and test waveforms. The 16-site option illustrates known population, not the authors’ unknown occupied pin numbers.

    No measured spectrum, proprietary mask files, actual connector map, or calibrated local recipe is represented. No nanowire transistor branch, biological response, administration, or deployment instructions are included.

    Bench solver

    A graph computes independent paths from fixture pins through physical pad overlaps to the Pt sites. A dry route is built from sheet resistance, route geometry and a contact estimate; a wet result additionally requires an exposed window.

    A constant-phase interface is anchored to one published mean at 1 kHz. Other frequencies, aperture and load behavior are extrapolations. Read model details.
