GLB preserves meshes, source names and basic physically based materials. Hologram shaders are converted to translucent materials. Visual glow, screen-only thickness assistance, clipping planes and interface labels are not baked into the GLB. Exports retain the full surfaces of the selected visible objects.

GLB units are meters: millimeters × 0.001; micrometers × 0.000001. Imported anatomy retains its source license; Allen data retains its data-use terms. Exporting does not turn generated membrane surfaces into measured or solver-ready tissue.
