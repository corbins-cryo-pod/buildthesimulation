EVIDENCE LEDGER

What is known

Primary device report
STIMEP / TIME-4H, Sensors (2021), §2.2.2 & Fig. 2a: materials, silicon carrier, U-to-L fold, contact diameter, active pitch/stagger, 7 + 1 sites per side, MFI ceramic and 16-wire cable.
Earlier design, explicitly separate
Boretius et al. (2010): original TIME concept. Its generation does not validate the 4H masks, thresholds or any later process settings.
Dimensional consequence
A circular 80 µm aperture has area 0.005027 mm². A fully open 109-aperture ground exposes a nominal 0.5479 mm²: one conductor, not 109 outputs. Real electrochemical surface area is not measured by this calculation.
Source gaps
No author GDS, terminal numbering, shaft width/thickness, pad overlap, process recipes, line resistance, strain limit or measured EIS is inferred. Material names alone do not establish the order of every lithographic operation.
MODEL NOTEBOOK

What this bench computes

Conductor witness
A graph connects 16 fixture terminals through a ceramic node layer to 16 physical nets. An open removes an edge; a bridge joins two nets; a swapped harness transposes terminal edges. Shaft contacts and ground openings remain physical even after a break.
Blocked versus broken
Occlusion removes exposed area only, not the buried conductor. Zero overlap or zero unblocked holes yields an unavailable surface path. DC mapping is virtual continuity at an ideal witness terminal, not probing through polyimide.
Illustrative interface
For geometric area A (mm²), Zcontact = 1 / [A(1/25 + j2πf × 0.3 × 10⁻⁶)]. The two contacts are in series with a 150 Ω fixture resistor. Area-specific resistance (25 Ω·mm²), capacitance (0.3 µF/mm²) and fixture resistance are chosen teaching values. Both interfaces deliberately use the same constants. No fit to SIROF or return electrodes is claimed.
Scope of output
SIROF is documented for active sites; the ground surface finish is not specified here. The mechanical model does not automatically fracture a film. No neural response, surgical procedure, usable medical device, real measurement or copy of the source’s stimulation controller is produced.
