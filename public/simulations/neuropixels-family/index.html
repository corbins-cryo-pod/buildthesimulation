Evidence & limitations

What the result proves

A rejected selection violates a modeled constraint. A shape documented in a paper still is not a complete pin map. Budget-only allocations can fail real switch rules. All non-1.0 “lanes” below are consecutive fixture slots, not validated hardware ADC indices.

Nominal capacity includes reference-related positions where the hardware uses them. It does not predict how many biological channels are usable. The bench does not create IMRO files or send commands.
