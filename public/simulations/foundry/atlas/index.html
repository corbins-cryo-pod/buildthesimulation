How to read this atlas

Browse Devices for catalog entries, Routes for all process profiles, or Equipment to work backward from an instrument. Select a device to play or read its process traveler: the sequence of material handoffs, equipment candidates and checks. The original diagrams are schematic and skip incompatible operations. A catalog entry may use a shared family route; that is not its manufacturer’s recipe.

Equipment IDs identify entries in the virtual register. They do not establish access, qualification, yield, or clinical suitability. No workflow authorizes physical operation.
