BTS Schola Narrow, preview 1
Mechanically narrowed derivative of TeX Gyre Schola 2.609 by Boguslaw Jackowski and Janusz M. Nowacki, with upstream credits preserved in each font.
Glyph outlines, horizontal metrics and horizontal positioning are scaled to 80% width. Vertical dimensions are unchanged. Not an official TeX Gyre condensed face or an optically redrawn design.
Renamed font family: BTS Schola Narrow. Files: bts-schola-narrow-{regular,italic,bold,bolditalic}.otf.

Original unmodified fonts are retained in ../tex-gyre-schola/.
Source: https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre/schola/index_html
Reproduction: python3 scripts/fonts/build-schola-narrow.py (fonttools required).
