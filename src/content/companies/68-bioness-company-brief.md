Bioness made the StimRouter peripheral nerve stimulation system. Bioventus acquired Bioness on March 30, 2021.

## Funding and ownership

- Bioventus's release says Bioness was based in Valencia, California, and was founded by the Al Mann Foundation in 2004.
- Bioventus paid $45 million up front, with up to $65 million of contingent consideration tied to future performance (the release gives the contingent terms; they are not repeated in full here).
- Bioventus stated that Bioness generated about $40 million in revenue in 2020, with a loss from operations of about $14.0 million. Those are the acquirer's figures for the whole company, which also sold rehabilitation products, not only StimRouter.

## Products and regulatory status

- Bioventus said StimRouter had been implanted in more than 3,000 patients since 2017 and was sold in more than 10 countries.
