# Rice University, Yang lab

Rice's current faculty profile identifies Kaiyuan Yang as Associate Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering. He leads the Secure and Intelligent Micro-Systems (SIMS) Lab. The SIMS project page describes low-power implant electronics and magnetoelectric or optical power and data transfer, and lists the 2022 endovascular ME-BIT paper.

The [Rice University, Robinson lab](/companies/43-rice-magnetoelectric-bioelectronics-labs/) is a separate group. Other magnetoelectric hardware in the catalog is grouped on that entry because the sources fetched here name Robinson, not Yang, for it.

## Cataloged work

- [ME-BIT nerve stimulator, 2022](/devices/113-mebit-magnetoelectric-endovascular-stimulator/): both faculty appear in the primary paper. Its [animal application](/applications/114-mebit-rat-pig-nerve-stimulation-2022/) keeps direct contact, vascular leads and separate delivery demonstrations distinct.

## Evidence boundary

The project page describes research directions, not proof that every listed device has all these functions. No clinical treatment or regulatory approval is assigned. Map coordinates are omitted because a university address does not establish a precise laboratory location.
