# Rice University, Luan lab

Rice University's current faculty page identifies Lan Luan as Associate Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering. The lab describes multimodal, bidirectional interfaces combining long-lasting electrical recording, optical imaging and neuromodulation.

The [Rice University, Xie lab](/companies/47-rice-xie-lab/) is a separate group. The two labs are listed as collaborators on the cataloged papers below, not as one lab.

## Cataloged hardware

- [Modular NET high-density arrays](/devices/71-modular-net-high-density-arrays/): the 2022 primary paper includes Rice affiliations. Its rodent configurations and follow-up belong to that hardware, not a completed human clinical system.
- [StimNET stimulation thread](/devices/98-stimnet-ultraflexible-stimulation-thread/): the 2023 published paper reports a stimulation-specific polyimide and iridium-oxide design, with a linked mouse application and explicit failures and limits.

The [2017 UT Austin NET work](/companies/35-ut-austin-nanoelectronic-thread-lab-brief/) stays attributed to its original institution.

## Evidence boundary

The lab website describes research directions and goals. Long-term clinical usefulness, wireless integration and larger-scale human deployment are not treated as completed results. No clinical trial, regulatory approval or therapeutic outcome is assigned. Map coordinates are omitted because a university address does not establish a precise laboratory location.
