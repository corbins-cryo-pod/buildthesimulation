# University of Tübingen, Birbaumer group

Niels Birbaumer is last author of the 2022 Nature Communications paper, with an affiliation at the Institute of Medical Psychology and Behavioral Neurobiology, University of Tübingen. The same paper lists the Wyss Center in Geneva as an author affiliation; see the [Wyss Center brief](/companies/81-wyss-center-bio-and-neuroengineering/).

## Cataloged hardware and applications

- [Intracortical speller in completely locked-in ALS, 2022](/applications/154-completely-locked-in-als-intracortical-speller-2022/): two Blackrock arrays in motor cortex, one participant. Device context: [Utah microelectrode array](/devices/01-utah-microelectrode-array/).

## Program claims versus study evidence

One patient, with results reported as in the paper: 1.08 characters per minute, intelligible output on 44 of 107 speller days. The paper compares this unfavorably with other intracortical studies.

## Not public or not verified

Group size, current participants and funding are not stated in the source used here.
