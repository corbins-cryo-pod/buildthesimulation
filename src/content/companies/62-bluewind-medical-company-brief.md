BlueWind Medical makes the Revi System, a tibial neuromodulation implant for urgency incontinence. The company says it was founded in 2010 by the Rainbow Medical Group (company statement). Its August 2023 release is datelined Park City, Utah and names BlueWind Medical, Ltd.

## Products and regulatory status

- FDA granted a De Novo marketing authorization for Revi in August 2023 (announced August 17, 2023). Indication: symptoms of urgency incontinence alone or with urinary urgency.
- Pivotal evidence: OASIS, a prospective, multicenter, single-arm, open-label study of 151 women, with results presented at the American Urological Association 2023 meeting (company release). Details are on the linked device sheet.
