Arbor Neuroscience is the new name for Forest Neurotech, a nonprofit Focused Research Organization (FRO) within Convergent Research. <https://www.arborneuroscience.org/> Forest built the Forest 1, a wearable functional ultrasound imaging platform; Arbor's stated goal is to make neuromodulation predictable - pairing intervention with measurement of how brain circuits respond, in the same individuals over months, and building symptom-to-circuit models from those longitudinal data (company statement). <https://www.arborneuroscience.org/introducing-arbor.html>

### The rebrand (September 2026)

Forest Neurotech announced the rename in September 2026, alongside a leadership change: Philip Sabes - a Neuralink co-founder who left a UCSF faculty position in 2017 - joined as CEO, and Whitney Griggs joined the leadership team. <https://insidebci.com/news/2026-09-19-philip-sabes-arbor-neuroscience-shanghai-bci-inno-salon-fudan-forest-neurotech-rebrand/> In his introduction letter, Sabes frames the field's current practice as "poke and hope" - stimulate, then wait - and Arbor's thesis as "predict and treat": the bottleneck is understanding what a circuit does, not stimulation hardware (company statement). <https://www.arborneuroscience.org/introducing-arbor.html>

### The Forest 1 platform

Per the company's technology page: Forest 1 is a wearable functional ultrasound imaging (fUSI) system - an 8,960-element Ultrasound-on-Chip ASIC from Butterfly Network in a 5.1 x 2.9 x 1.5 cm probe, giving continuous volumetric imaging to 10 cm depth. fUSI tracks blood-flow changes driven by neural activity - the same class of signal as fMRI, at better spatial and temporal resolution. Because fUSI does not record effectively through the skull, the system images through a craniectomy or a sonolucent cranial implant (PMMA or PEEK); the company estimates tens of thousands of people in the US already have such an implant. A next version is planned to add focused-ultrasound neuromodulation, making the device bidirectional - stimulate and measure from one instrument (company statement). <https://www.arborneuroscience.org/technology.html>

### History as Forest Neurotech (2023-2026)

- Founded in 2023 by Sumner Norman, Tyson Aflalo and Will Biederman, as an FRO created by Convergent Research. <https://www.arborneuroscience.org/about.html>
- October 24, 2023: five-year co-development agreement with Butterfly Network (NYSE: BFLY) covering Butterfly's Ultrasound-on-Chip technology, with $20 million in licensing, chip, services and milestone payments to Butterfly ($3.5 million received on signing), per Butterfly's press release. The stated goal was an implanted, whole-brain neural interface. <https://www.butterflynetwork.com/press-releases/co-development-agreement-with-forest-neurotech>
- March 2024: a $14 million funding commitment from former Google CEO Eric Schmidt, channelled through Convergent Research; earlier funders named in the same release include Griffin Catalyst (Kenneth C. Griffin), the Riley & Susan Bechtel Foundation and James Fickel. <https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240311581698/en/Convergent-Research-Announces-Major-New-Funding-Commitment-to-Forest-Neurotech>
- Biederman told IEEE Spectrum in 2023 that, as a nonprofit research organization, Forest had no plans to submit to the FDA within its lifetime, and intended to make the technology available to other research groups. <https://spectrum.ieee.org/bci-ultrasound>
- The founders have since moved to new ventures: Norman and Aflalo co-founded Merge Labs, and Biederman founded [Bridge Neurotech](/companies/89-bridge-neurotech-company-brief/), which is building a fully non-invasive through-skull fUSI BCI. <https://insidebci.com/directory/arbor-neuroscience/>

### First clinical study (UK, ARIA-funded)

Arbor's first human study is underway with the Barking, Havering and Redbridge University Hospitals NHS Trust and the University of Plymouth, funded by a £6.5 million award from ARIA (Advanced Research and Invention Agency) within its £69 million Precision Neurotechnologies programme. <https://www.bhrhospitals.nhs.uk/news/65m-award-brain-changer-4960/> The study runs three and a half years from March 2025 and will recruit up to 30 participants with prior craniectomies; the device is placed on the skin at the skull-defect site. Objectives are the safety and tolerability of Forest 1 plus measuring and modulating brain activity to develop a predictive model of affective (mood-related) brain states, led by Aimun Jamjoom (BHR) with Elsa Fouragnan (Plymouth) as co-investigator. <https://www.plymouth.ac.uk/research/psychology/brain-research-and-imaging-centre/transcranial-ultrasound-stimulation/next-generation-brain-machine-interface-for-whole-brain-input-output> <https://aria.org.uk/insights/2025/funding-world-leading-research-into-new-neurotechnologies>

### What to watch

1. First published human fUSI results from the UK study - the test of the predict-and-treat thesis.
2. The planned bidirectional version: whether adding focused-ultrasound neuromodulation closes the loop from measurement to intervention.
3. Funding and structure beyond the first phase - the organization says it has sized phase one to core funding in place; current total funding is not disclosed. <https://www.arborneuroscience.org/introducing-arbor.html>
