# Case Western Reserve University and the Cleveland FES Center of Excellence

The 2017 Lancet paper lists A. Bolu Ajiboye of the Department of Biomedical Engineering, Case Western Reserve University, as corresponding author. Its author affiliations include the School of Medicine at Case Western and the Louis Stokes Cleveland VA Medical Center, Functional Electrical Stimulation Center of Excellence, Rehabilitation R&D Service.

## Cataloged hardware and applications

- [iBCI-controlled FES for reaching and grasping, 2017](/applications/155-cwru-ibci-controlled-fes-reach-grasp-2017/): two intracortical arrays and 36 percutaneous muscle electrodes in one participant. Device context: [Utah microelectrode array](/devices/01-utah-microelectrode-array/); related program: [BrainGate consortium](/companies/13-braingate-consortium-lab-brief/).

## Program claims versus study evidence

Results are from one participant in a proof-of-concept study, as the authors describe it.

## Not public or not verified

Current studies, participants and funding beyond the NIH and VA statement in the paper were not read here.
