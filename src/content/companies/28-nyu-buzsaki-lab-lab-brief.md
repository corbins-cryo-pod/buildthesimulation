The *Buzsaki Lab* sits in the NYU Neuroscience Institute at NYU Langone Medical Center, New York. György Buzsáki, M.D., Ph.D., is listed on the lab's contact page as Biggs Professor of Neuroscience.

- Lab site: <https://buzsakilab.com/wp/>
- Contact and address: <https://buzsakilab.com/wp/contact/>

### Device entries

- [NeuroGrid](/devices/24-neurogrid-pedot-pss-surface-array/): 4 µm thick PEDOT:PSS array with 10 by 10 µm sites on 30 µm pitch. The 2015 paper lists Buzsáki as the final author, with Malliaras, Devinsky and others.

### Limits

NeuroGrid was a collaboration across institutions, so this brief does not claim the device as a single-lab product.
