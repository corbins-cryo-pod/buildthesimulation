Inspire Medical Systems makes the Inspire upper airway stimulation system for obstructive sleep apnea. It is a public company (NYSE: INSP) based in Minneapolis, and says it was formed in 2007 when its technology was spun out of Medtronic (company statement).

## Products and regulatory status

- FY2024 10-K: FDA premarket approval for Inspire therapy in 2014, a CE certificate in the EU in 2010, and Japanese approval in 2018, with Japan national health insurance listing in 2021.
- The latest generation, Inspire V, was launched in 2025. The company's February 2026 results release headline reports FDA approval for 3.0 T MRI compatibility and completion of the Inspire V launch (company statement).

## Financials (company-reported)

- Fourth-quarter 2025 revenue was $269.1 million, up 12%, with operating margin of 17.1% (company release). The full-year figure was not read here.
