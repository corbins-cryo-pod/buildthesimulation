# Cornell University, Molnar lab

The 2025 primary paper lists Alyosha Molnar with Cornell electrical and computer engineering. Cornell's November 2025 report names Molnar and Sunwoo Lee as co-leads and describes Lee's earlier postdoctoral work in Molnar's lab. The paper lists Lee with Nanyang Technological University and KAIST.

The [Cornell University, Xu group](/companies/49-cornell-xu-group/) is listed separately as a collaborator. The paper also lists Boston University and University of Arizona collaborators, and further Cornell departments. Those are the paper's affiliations, not inferred ownership of each device component.

## Cataloged hardware

The [2025 MOTE recorder](/devices/135-mote-optoelectronic-tetherless-recorder-2025/) combines a CMOS electrical recorder with an AlGaAs photovoltaic/LED element, compact encapsulation and optical telemetry. The [mouse application](/applications/136-mote-mouse-cortical-recording-2025/) reports surface and shallow intracortical measurements under a cranial window, one weakened averaged LFP response at day 365, and failed devices and implant drift. It is not a human product, clinical trial or approval.
