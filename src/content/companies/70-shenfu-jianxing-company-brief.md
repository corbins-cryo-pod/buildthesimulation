神复健行(上海)医疗器械有限公司 (Shenfu Jianxing, Shanghai) is a Fudan University translation company built around Jia Fumin's team at Fudan's Institute of Science and Technology for Brain-Inspired Intelligence. Its product is a brain-spine interface: brain electrodes decode motor intent and drive epidural stimulation of the spinal cord in paraplegia. The linked device sheet carries the hardware detail; most hardware cells there are blank because the specifications are not published.

## Products and regulatory status

- Investigator-initiated clinical study, not approved. Four patients were implanted between January 8 and March 3, 2025 at Zhongshan Hospital and Huashan Hospital in Shanghai (hospital and university releases, in Chinese).
- Fudan reported on December 12, 2025 that the product entered the US FDA Breakthrough Therapy pathway (university statement; the FDA designation itself was not verified here).
- The team said in March 2025 that it planned third-party type testing and registration-trial preparation.
- A study is registered in the Chinese Clinical Trial Registry as ChiCTR2500095359.

## Funding and ownership

Fudan and People's Daily Shanghai describe the company as arising from the Fudan team. Investors, funding rounds and shareholding are not stated in the sources used here.
