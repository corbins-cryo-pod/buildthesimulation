神复健行(上海)医疗器械有限公司 is a Shanghai startup built around Jia Fumin's team at Fudan University's Institute of Science and Technology for Brain-Inspired Intelligence. Its brain-spine interface product is reported by Fudan and People's Daily Shanghai to have entered the FDA Breakthrough Therapy pathway.
