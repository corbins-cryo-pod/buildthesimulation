*LivaNova USA, Inc.* is the applicant listed on the FDA premarket approval record for the VNS Therapy System, P970003, at 100 Cyberonics Blvd, Houston, Texas. The same approval documents list the earlier applicant as Cyberonics, Inc.

- FDA record: <https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P970003>
- Company site: <https://www.livanova.com/>

### Regulatory trail (FDA records)

- 16 July 1997: approval for epilepsy.
- 15 July 2005: notice of approval for treatment-resistant depression (supplement S50).

### Limits

The FDA record does not say when or how Cyberonics became LivaNova, so this brief does not state it.
