LivaNova USA, Inc. is the applicant listed on the FDA premarket approval record for the VNS Therapy System, P970003, LivaNova PLC was created by the cross-border merger of Sorin S.p.A. and Cyberonics, Inc., completed October 19, 2015 (company release), and trades on Nasdaq and the London Stock Exchange under LIVN.

## Products and regulatory status

- The FDA premarket approval record P970003 covers the VNS Therapy System. The current device is on the linked sheet.
- February 2026 results release (company statement): the CMS reimbursement for VNS Therapy procedures increased effective January 1, 2026.

## Financials (company-reported)

- Full-year 2025 revenue was $1.39 billion, up 10.7% as reported. This is total company revenue; the neuromodulation share was not read here.
