ABILITY Neurotech (Geneva, Campus Biotech) is developing a *fully implantable communication BCI* for people with severe paralysis. Per the company's study page, the implant captures neural activity through *4 electrode arrays on the brain surface* and wirelessly transmits it for decoding into computer commands.

### INTRECOM clinical study (recruiting)

The INTRECOM study runs at *UMC Utrecht* with Medical Research Ethics Committee approval. Per the company:
- participants: ages 18 to 70 with severe paralysis and speech problems from traumatic brain injury, stroke, neurodegenerative or neuromuscular disease (including ALS); still able to communicate by other means such as eye blinking; cognitively unimpaired
- the study lasts about one year, with home-based training and a goal of independent home use
- clinical lead: Dr Mariska Vansteensel, Associate Professor at UMC Utrecht

- ABILITY study page: <https://abilityneuro.com/clinical-trial/>

Trade press reported on Oct 12-13, 2026 that the company had begun first-in-human testing of its implant. The company's own study page confirms the study is recruiting but does not state an implant date, so the first-in-human milestone is noted here as reported only.

- Medical Product Outsourcing (Oct 12, 2026): <https://www.mpo-mag.com/breaking-news/ability-neurotech-begins-testing-its-brain-computer-interface-technology/>
