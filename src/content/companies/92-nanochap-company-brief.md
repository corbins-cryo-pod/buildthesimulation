Nanochap Electronics (暖芯迦, Nuanxinjia) is a Hangzhou-based brain-computer interface and biochip company founded in February 2014, focused on restoring vision for people with blindness. It pursues a dual-pathway strategy: E-BCI, an electronic retinal implant, and V-BCI, an implant targeting the visual cortex (company statement). <https://www.nanochap.com/about.html> Co-founder and CEO Yang Jiawei was previously a researcher at the University of Melbourne's neural engineering centre and worked on chip design for Australia's national bionic eye project; the company also runs research centres in Melbourne and Ho Chi Minh City. <https://insidebci.com/market/2026-11-04-nanochap-rmb-500-million-series-c-plus-gaorong-retinal-vision-bci-hangzhou/>

### E-BCI retinal implant

E-BCI pairs a 3 x 3 mm implant carrying a 320-channel stimulation chip and high-density electrode array with a wearable kit: camera glasses, a video processing unit, and a coupling coil that transmits power and data wirelessly (company specifications). The company claims hermetic packaging with a designed lifetime of more than 10 years and an in-house implant production line in a clean room of nearly 2,000 square metres. The system is intended for people blinded by retinal disease who retain an intact eye structure, optic nerve and visual cortical pathways. <https://www.nanochap.com/product/1.html> <https://insidebci.com/market/2026-11-04-nanochap-rmb-500-million-series-c-plus-gaorong-retinal-vision-bci-hangzhou/>

### Registration trial (2026)

- May 19, 2026: surgeons at Zhejiang Provincial People's Hospital implanted the first patient, days after Nanochap announced a RMB 300 million round earmarked for clinical trials. <https://insidebci.com/market/2026-05-29-tencent-takes-stake-nanochap-bci-chip-hangzhou/>
- A registration trial - the stage that generates data for an approval application to China's regulator, the NMPA - is underway at Zhejiang Provincial People's Hospital, the Hangzhou campus of Wenzhou Medical University Eye Hospital, and Renmin Hospital of Wuhan University. Nanochap says three patients have been implanted using different surgical approaches; the hospitals publicised the cases in July, August and October without naming the company, and no outcomes have been published in a peer-reviewed journal. Inside BCI reports that a 40-year-old woman with retinitis pigmentosa began recognising letters within days of her implant being switched on in early June (company-reported). <https://insidebci.com/market/2026-11-04-nanochap-rmb-500-million-series-c-plus-gaorong-retinal-vision-bci-hangzhou/>
- Nanochap says it aims to complete 30 implants in 2026 and to seek commercial approval in 2027. Total trial enrollment has not been disclosed.

### V-BCI visual-cortex implant

V-BCI places a flexible electrode array in the subdural space over the visual cortex without penetrating brain tissue, bypassing the retina and optic nerve; it carries 1,280 recording and 1,280 stimulation channels (company specifications). It targets people who have lost an eye or have severe optic-nerve damage. The product is in animal studies; a first-in-human trial planned for 2026 has not been reported. <https://www.nanochap.com/product/2.html> <https://insidebci.com/market/2026-11-04-nanochap-rmb-500-million-series-c-plus-gaorong-retinal-vision-bci-hangzhou/>

- Angel round October 2014; Series A 2016; Series A+ 2018; Series B 2021; Series B+ 2024 (company milestones). <https://www.nanochap.com/about.html>
- May 6, 2026: RMB 300 million strategic round co-led by Legend Capital and an unnamed industrial investor, earmarked for clinical trials and core technology development. <https://insidebci.com/market/2026-11-04-nanochap-rmb-500-million-series-c-plus-gaorong-retinal-vision-bci-hangzhou/>
- May 2026: a Tencent subsidiary, Shanghai Qishan Investment, appeared on Nanochap's shareholder register; the stake's size and value have not been disclosed. <https://insidebci.com/market/2026-05-29-tencent-takes-stake-nanochap-bci-chip-hangzhou/>
- November 3, 2026: RMB 500 million Series C+ led by Gaorong Ventures, with PICC Equity, Monolith, C&D Emerging Industry Investment, Orient Jiafu, Danhe Capital and Hangzhou Industrial Investment Group participating; existing shareholders including Legend Capital increased their stakes beyond pro-rata. Cumulative funding reaches RMB 1 billion. ### Chip business and certifications

Beyond vision implants, Nanochap sells nerve-stimulation, biosensing and EEG-detection chips for consumer health, rehabilitation and research products such as massage, beauty-care and smart-home devices. <https://insidebci.com/market/2026-05-29-tencent-takes-stake-nanochap-bci-chip-hangzhou/> The company reports ISO 13485 certification (2022), National High-Tech Enterprise status (2022), National-Level "Little Giant" Enterprise recognition (2024), and founding membership of the Brain-Computer Interface Subcommittee of China's National Information Technology Standardization Committee (2025) (company statements). <https://www.nanochap.com/about.html>

### What to watch

1. Whether Nanochap reaches 30 E-BCI implants by the end of 2026 and files for NMPA approval in 2027 as planned.
2. Peer-reviewed publication of E-BCI trial outcomes - all efficacy data so far is company-reported.
3. Whether the V-BCI cortical implant reaches a first human patient.
4. Disclosure of the Tencent stake size and of how the Series C+ funds will be spent.
