Neuros Medical is a privately held company in Aliso Viejo, California, working on post-amputation pain with the Altius high-frequency nerve block system (company statement).

## Products and regulatory status

- The company's July 2025 release describes the Altius Direct Electrical Nerve Stimulation System as FDA-approved for chronic intractable phantom and residual lower limb post-amputation pain in adult amputees, with patient-controlled, on-demand relief (company statement). The FDA record is P230020, cited on the device sheet.
- The manufacturer's patient manual says MRI safety has not been tested, which the device sheet records.

## Funding and ownership

- July 9, 2025: closed an oversubscribed $56 million Series D led by new investor EQT Life Sciences, with existing investors including US Venture Partners, Amzak Health, Osage University Partners, Sectoral Asset Management and Aperture Venture Partners. Proceeds are for US commercialization (company release).
