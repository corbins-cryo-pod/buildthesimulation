智冉医疗 says it was founded in 2022 and develops flexible invasive brain-computer interfaces; it lists an address in Haidian District, Beijing (company statement, Chinese).

- Company site (Chinese): <https://bciflex.com/>

### Financing and development

The company's February 11, 2026 news headline reports an RMB 300 million Series A+ round led by CAS Star, with existing investors participating. Its May 19 announcement describes the over-100-channel multicenter GCP trial; the [LEAP device entry](/devices/184-zhiran-ultra-100-channel-flexible-bci-leap/) documents the May 18 launch and hardware.

A separate May 21 company announcement says its intracranial deep electrode entered the innovative-device review “green channel.” This concerns the deep-electrode product, not approval of the LEAP BCI system. Source for these company statements: [Zhiran company news](https://bciflex.com/News?page=0).
