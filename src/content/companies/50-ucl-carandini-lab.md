# University College London, Carandini lab

The paper's author information names Matteo Carandini as joint supervisor with Joshua Siegle of the [Allen Institute](/companies/46-allen-ucl-neuropixels-opto-collaboration/). The UCL program page says the development team is led by Siegle at Allen and Carandini at UCL. The fetched sources name Carandini at UCL but not a lab name, so the lab title here follows the group leader. The [IMEC](/companies/51-imec/) entry covers the Belgian partner.

No funding amount, price, licence or corporate ownership is inferred.

## Prototype status

The [2026 Neuropixels Opto prototype](/devices/107-neuropixels-opto-photonic-prototype/) is a prototype, not a product release. UCL expects availability to the community in 2028; that is a forecast, not current availability or a price. Applications: [paper overview](/applications/108-neuropixels-opto-mouse-optotagging-2026/), [cortical activation and inhibition](/applications/141-neuropixels-opto-cortical-activation-inhibition/), [parallel optotagging](/applications/142-neuropixels-opto-parallel-optotagging/).
