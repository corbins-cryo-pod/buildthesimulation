# Cornell University, Xu group

The 2025 primary paper lists Chris Xu with Cornell applied and engineering physics. The Xu Research Group publishes a project page for the paper, with its author list.

The [Cornell University, Molnar lab](/companies/44-cornell-mote-neurotechnology-collaboration/) is a separate group and the paper's named co-lead lab. This entry does not assign particular circuits, optics or animal studies to the Xu group beyond what the paper's affiliations show.

## Cataloged hardware

[MOTE optoelectronic tetherless recorder, 2025](/devices/135-mote-optoelectronic-tetherless-recorder-2025/) and its [mouse application](/applications/136-mote-mouse-cortical-recording-2025/), which keeps failed devices and drift visible. No human use is reported.
