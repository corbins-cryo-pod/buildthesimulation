SetPoint Medical makes the SetPoint System, an implanted vagus nerve stimulator for rheumatoid arthritis. The company says it was founded in 2007 to commercialize therapies for chronic autoimmune conditions (company statement). It is in Valencia, California and is privately held.

## Products and regulatory status

- FDA approved the SetPoint System on July 31, 2025 (company announcement), the PMA record being P240039. The company describes it as the first neuroimmune modulation therapy for rheumatoid arthritis.
- The approval rested on the 242-patient randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled RESET-RA study (company statement). The system had FDA Breakthrough Device Designation.
- The company says it plans to evaluate the platform in additional autoimmune indications including multiple sclerosis and Crohn's disease. These are plans, not trials read here.

## Funding and ownership

- August 11, 2025: SetPoint announced $140 million in private financing, $25 million as the second tranche of its Series C and $115 million in Series D, co-led by Elevage Medical Technologies and Ally Bridge Group. Investors named in the release include Abbott and Boston Scientific.
