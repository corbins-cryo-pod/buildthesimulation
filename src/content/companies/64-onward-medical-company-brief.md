ONWARD Medical develops spinal cord stimulation therapies for movement after spinal cord injury. It is a public company (Euronext: ONWD).

## Products and regulatory status

- ARC-EX is an external spinal stimulation system; ARC-IM is the implantable system covered on the linked device sheet.
- FY2025 results release (March 31, 2026): ARC-EX received a CE mark for clinic and home use, and the FDA granted a 510(k) clearance in November 2025 to expand the ARC-EX indication to home use in the US. The FDA also approved an investigational device exemption (IDE) for ARC-IM, allowing the Empower BP global pivotal study to start (company statements).

## Financials (company-reported)

- 2025 revenue of EUR 5.4 million; EUR 50.8 million of equity raised in October 2025; year-end net cash position of EUR 68.1 million.

## Not public or not verified

- Headcount and the per-product revenue split were not read here. ARC-IM is investigational and not marketed.
