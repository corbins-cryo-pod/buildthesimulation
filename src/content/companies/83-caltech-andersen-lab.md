# Caltech, Andersen lab

Richard A. Andersen is last author of the 2024 Nature Human Behaviour study. The paper's record lists the California Institute of Technology, Division of Biology and Biological Engineering, and the T&C Chen Brain-Machine Interface Center as author affiliations, along with the Keck School of Medicine of USC and Rancho Los Amigos National Rehabilitation Center.

## Cataloged hardware and applications

- [Internal speech in human supramarginal gyrus, 2024](/applications/156-caltech-internal-speech-smg-single-neurons-2024/): arrays in supramarginal gyrus and somatosensory cortex in two participants.

## Program claims versus study evidence

Results are from two participants with very different decoding accuracy (79% and 23% online). Earlier Caltech work on parietal cortex is referenced in the paper but is not cataloged here.

## Not public or not verified

Array manufacturer, regulatory pathway, funding and other participants were not read from the source used here.
