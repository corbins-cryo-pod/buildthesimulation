Medtronic plc is a large public medical technology company (NYSE: MDT). The device sheets linked here cover its neuromodulation systems.

## Products and regulatory status

- Approvals and labeling for its neuromodulation systems are on the linked sheets.
- Medtronic announced on May 20, 2026 its intent to acquire SPR Therapeutics for about $650 million upfront and completed the acquisition on July 16, 2026, adding SPR's SPRINT short-term peripheral nerve stimulation system to its neuromodulation portfolio (company releases). SPRINT has its own sheet in the catalog.

## Financials (company-reported)

- Fiscal 2026: worldwide revenue of $36.364 billion, up 8.4% as reported and 5.8% organic. Fourth-quarter revenue was $9.807 billion. The neuromodulation share is reported inside segment tables not read here.
