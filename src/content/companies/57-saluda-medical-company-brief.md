Saluda Medical makes the Evoke closed-loop spinal cord stimulation system for chronic pain. The FDA record lists the applicant in Artarmon, New South Wales, Australia; the company's January 2025 financing release is datelined Minneapolis, Minnesota. Which is the operating headquarters is not stated in the sources read.

## Products and regulatory status

- FDA approved the Evoke PMA (P190002) on February 28, 2022.
- The company describes Evoke as a physiologic closed-loop, dose-control system that senses neural responses to stimulation and adjusts therapy automatically (company statement). The system is covered on the linked device sheet.

## Funding and ownership

- January 10, 2025: closed a $100 million financing led by existing investor Redmile Group, with Wellington Management, TPG Life Sciences Innovation, Fidelity, Action Potential Venture Capital and T. Rowe Price advised funds, plus new investor Piper Heartland Healthcare Capital. Proceeds are for commercializing Evoke (company release).
- Earlier: a AU$53 million Series D announced in May 2017 (PR Newswire headline; terms not read here).
