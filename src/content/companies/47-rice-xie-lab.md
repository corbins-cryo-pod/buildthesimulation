# Rice University, Xie lab

Rice University's current faculty page identifies Chong Xie as Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering and Neuroengineering. The lab describes nanoscale neural interfaces, and Rice's faculty profile lists ultraflexible NET recording, high-density acquisition and intracortical microstimulation as research directions.

The [Rice University, Luan lab](/companies/40-rice-ultraflexible-neural-interface-labs/) is a separate group that appears with this lab on the cataloged work.

## Cataloged hardware

- [Modular NET high-density arrays](/devices/71-modular-net-high-density-arrays/): the 2022 primary paper includes Rice affiliations.
- [StimNET stimulation thread](/devices/98-stimnet-ultraflexible-stimulation-thread/): the 2023 paper and its [mouse application](/applications/99-stimnet-chronic-mouse-microstimulation-2023/) keep failures and limits visible.

Earlier NET work from his time in Texas stays with its original institution: see the [UT Austin entry](/companies/35-ut-austin-nanoelectronic-thread-lab-brief/).

## Evidence boundary

The lab website describes research directions. No clinical trial, regulatory approval or therapeutic outcome is assigned. Map coordinates are omitted because a university address does not establish a precise laboratory location.
