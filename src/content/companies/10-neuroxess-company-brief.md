NeuroXess is a Shanghai-based BCI company mentioned in reporting about China’s push to build an implantable BCI industry.

### Reported status (Wired)

Wired reports that:
- NeuroXess’ CEO Phoenix Peng says the company has *implanted six* paralyzed patients
- in three patients, the system was used to *decode Chinese speech*
- in others, it enabled *thought control of digital devices*

- Wired: “China Is Building a Brain-Computer Interface Industry” (policy + company quotes):
  <https://www.wired.com/story/china-is-getting-serious-about-brain-computer-interfaces/>

### Why it’s worth tracking

China’s BCI ecosystem is increasingly driven by:
- centralized policy roadmaps (2027/2030 milestones)
- rapid trial starts
- building a local supply chain (chips, electrodes, decoding algorithms)

NeuroXess is one of the clearest named examples in mainstream reporting with multi-patient implant claims.

### What to watch

1) *Peer-reviewed publications / technical details*
Electrode type, channel count, implantation approach, and decoding pipeline.

2) *Regulatory pathway*
What approvals exist now, and what is the path to a marketed medical device?

3) *Performance metrics*
Accuracy, throughput, training time, stability over months.

### 2026 update (reviewed Oct 4, 2026)

- *Status (reported by the company):* NeuroXess says its fully implanted, fully wireless system started a GCP registration clinical trial at Huashan Hospital on Jul 8, 2026, which it describes as the first subdural implantable BCI in China to reach registration-stage trials. The company reported a second clinical implant in Apr 2026. The "six patients" figure above is from earlier Wired reporting. Sources (company, in Chinese): <https://www.neuroxess.com/news/naohukejisanquannaojijiekouxitongzhengshiqidonggcpzhucelinchuangshiyan/> and <https://www.neuroxess.com/news/kejixiangshan/>
- *Triple-F participant update (Sep 30, 2026, company news):* NeuroXess disclosed that a participant with a C4 spinal cord injury (pseudonym Jia), implanted with the Triple-F system in April 2026, used brain control with an AI large model and 3D printing to make a figurine and a custom finger sleeve, and used AI smart glasses with its XessOS software to control a hospital bed, lights and home devices. This is a company-reported exploration, not a trial result. Source: <https://en.neuroxess.com/news/neuroxess-pronounced-latest-progress-in-triple-f-bci-from-what-you-think-is-what-you-get-to-what-you-see-is-what-you-control/>
- *Registration trial record:* ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT07720882 (NeuroXess Technology (Shanghai) as lead sponsor), recruiting, actual start Jul 7, 2026, estimated enrollment 32, estimated primary completion Jan 30, 2027. This is one day earlier than the Jul 8 start the company gives above. Source: <https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07720882>. Inside BCI (secondary) reports Huashan Hospital as collaborator, a primary endpoint of ARAT response rate at three months, and that the Sep 30 demonstrations are not among the trial's listed outcomes: <https://insidebci.com/news/2026-10-02-neuroxess-triple-full-bci-ai-3d-printed-figurine-smart-glasses-huashan-registration-trial/>
