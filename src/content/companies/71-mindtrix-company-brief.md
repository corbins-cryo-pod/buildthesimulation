明视脑机科技(苏州)有限公司 (Mindtrix, Suzhou) is developing a visual cortex prosthesis for blind patients. Founder and CEO Liu Bing is an associate researcher at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and the company was founded in October 2024 (Jiemian; China Science Daily via CAS).

## Products and regulatory status

- System as described on the company site and in press: smart glasses with a camera, a handheld video processing unit, a wireless implanted stimulator module and a cortical-surface electrode array. The company product page lists a 128-channel dual-function recording and stimulation electrode (company claim).
- December 2025: the team reported visual percepts of complex shapes and colors in one patient in an investigator-initiated study (company claim, reported by China Science Daily). The patient had temporary therapeutic electrodes from epilepsy surgery, so the study did not use the company's own implant.
- Status: investigator-initiated trial stage, no approval. The founder said the team plans type testing in 2026 and a registration trial for blind patients in 2028 (plans, not results).

## Funding and ownership

Jiemian reported cumulative financing of 150 million yuan in April 2026 (press report). Investors and round structure are not stated in the sources used here.
