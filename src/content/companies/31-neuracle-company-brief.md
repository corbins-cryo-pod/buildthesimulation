*Neuracle* is the sponsor of the NEO clinical trial. The ClinicalTrials.gov record lists the lead sponsor as Neuracle Medical Technology (Shanghai) Co., Ltd.

- Trial record: <https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06990412>
- Company site: <https://www.neuracle.cn/>

### The NEO trial (registry record)

- Official title: a prospective, multi-center clinical trial of the implantable wireless BCI system NEO in patients with tetraplegia, to provide a basis for product registration.
- Status Completed. Start 28 May 2025, primary completion 6 November 2025, completion 10 January 2026.
- Collaborating hospitals include Huashan Hospital, Xuanwu Hospital Beijing, Beijing Tiantan Hospital and West China Hospital, among others.

### Commercialization

NEO received NMPA approval on March 13, 2026 for hand-grasp compensation in adults with cervical spinal cord injury. The approved indication and component specifications are detailed in the [NEO device entry](/devices/41-neo-neuracle-tsinghua-epidural-bci/).

Neuracle's September 29, 2026 Shanghai Stock Exchange reply reports 13 NEO-ONE SCI sales and eight terminal implants since July, with more than 70 orders in hand or intended. Its proposed IPO would issue at most 20 million shares and allocate RMB 2.5 billion in proceeds, including RMB 1.54 billion for BCI R&D. These are proposed financing figures, not funds already raised. The filing's roughly RMB 200,000 ex-factory price is a consultancy forecast, not a confirmed selling price. Source: [SSE filing, page 8-1-136](https://static.sse.com.cn/stock/disclosure/announcement/c/202609/002198_20260929_9IJE.pdf).

### Access and regulation

NHSA created separate service-price items for invasive BCI implantation, removal and noninvasive fitting on March 11, 2025. Its explainer also records a January 2025 NMPA standard for EEG dataset quality and evaluation in BCI AI algorithms; the standard itself was not reviewed. Source: [NHSA service-price guide](https://www.nhsa.gov.cn/art/2025/3/12/art_14_15948.html).

NHSA assigned the Neuracle product an insurance classification code on March 15, 2026, reported March 22. A code enables identification within the insurance system; it does not establish universal reimbursement. Source: [NHSA product-code announcement](https://www.nhsa.gov.cn/art/2026/3/22/art_14_19989.html).

NMPA's June 30, 2026 guidance covers active medical devices that measure CNS signals, decode them in real time and provide two-way interaction or closed-loop feedback to improve, repair or replace CNS function. Notice 2026 No. 24 is dated June 29 and takes effect on publication. Invasive or implantable BCI devices are Class III, code 12-00. Noninvasive treatment systems are Class III, 09-00; function-compensation and rehabilitation systems are Class III, 19-00, except stroke limb rehabilitation without AI, which is Class II, 19-00. One-way recording or stimulation, peripheral-only signals including EMG, and nonmedical products fall outside this guidance. Generic names combine two or three feature terms with a core term; the hand-function compensation system is a naming example. Sources: [NMPA notice via NIFDC](https://www.nidc.org.cn/xxgk/zcfg/flfg/202607011610522105080.html), [NMPA classification and naming explanation](https://bk.cnpharm.com/zgyyb/2026/07/09/app_325363.html), [Shanghai regulator naming explanation](https://yjj.sh.gov.cn/zcjd/20260701/f8cd5ee49b64479f8eed24c0f764ff96.html). The original guidance attachments were not reviewed.

### Additional trial record

[ChiCTR2500102814](https://www.chictr.org.cn/showproj.html?proj=273107), registered May 20, 2025, describes a single-arm interventional study funded by Neuracle, with 32 protocol participants aged 18–65. It names Mao Ying at Huashan and Zhao Guoguang at Xuanwu, with planned dates May 20, 2025–May 20, 2026. These are registry planning dates, distinct from the actual ClinicalTrials.gov dates above and the approved age range of 18–60.
