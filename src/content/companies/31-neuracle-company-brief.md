*Neuracle* is the sponsor of the NEO clinical trial. The ClinicalTrials.gov record lists the lead sponsor as Neuracle Medical Technology (Shanghai) Co., Ltd.

- Trial record: <https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06990412>
- Company site: <https://www.neuracle.cn/>

### The NEO trial (registry record)

- Official title: a prospective, multi-center clinical trial of the implantable wireless BCI system NEO in patients with tetraplegia, to provide a basis for product registration.
- Status Completed. Start 28 May 2025, primary completion 6 November 2025, completion 10 January 2026.
- Collaborating hospitals include Huashan Hospital, Xuanwu Hospital Beijing, Beijing Tiantan Hospital and West China Hospital, among others.
