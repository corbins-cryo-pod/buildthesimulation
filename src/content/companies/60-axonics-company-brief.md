Axonics made a sacral neuromodulation system for bladder and bowel dysfunction. Boston Scientific closed its acquisition of Axonics on November 15, 2024.

## Products and regulatory status

- The system is covered on the linked device sheet, including the FDA PMA P190006 summary used there.

## Funding and ownership

- Boston Scientific's closing release gives a purchase price of $71 per share in cash, an equity value of $3.7 billion and an enterprise value of $3.3 billion.
- Before the deal, Axonics was a public company (Nasdaq: AXNX) based in Irvine, California. For fiscal 2023 it reported net revenue of $366.4 million, up 34% (company release), and said clinicians used its therapies to treat about 100,000 incontinence patients that year (company claim).
