MED-EL is a cochlear implant maker headquartered in Innsbruck, Austria.

## Products and regulatory status

- The FDA PMA P000025, with its S110 supplement for the Mi1250 SYNCHRONY 2 cochlear implant, lists the applicant as Med-El Corp. at Furstenweg 77a, Innsbruck; the supplement was received March 6, 2019. The system is covered on the linked device sheet.
