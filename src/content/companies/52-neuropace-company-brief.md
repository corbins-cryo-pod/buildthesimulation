NeuroPace is a publicly traded medical device company (Nasdaq: NPCE) in Mountain View, California, focused on epilepsy. Its RNS System is a brain-responsive neurostimulator that records brain activity and delivers stimulation in response.

## Products and regulatory status

- FDA granted premarket approval for the RNS System for medically refractory epilepsy on November 14, 2013 (NeuroPace announcement). The FDA PMA record is P100026 and lists NeuroPace, Inc., Mountain View, as applicant.
- In its fourth-quarter 2025 release, NeuroPace said it had submitted a PMA supplement seeking an expanded indication for idiopathic generalized epilepsy, citing 18-month NAUTILUS trial results (company-reported 77% median seizure reduction), and had submitted Seizure ID software to the FDA for review. Neither was approved in the sources read.

## Financials (company-reported)

- Total revenue for 2025 was $100.0 million, up 25% from $79.9 million in 2024. RNS System sales were $81.7 million. Guidance for 2026 was $98 million to $100 million.
