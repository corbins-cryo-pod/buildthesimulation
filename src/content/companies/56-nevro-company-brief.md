Nevro made the Senza family of spinal cord stimulation systems, including the Senza HFX iQ, for chronic pain. Globus Medical completed its acquisition of Nevro on April 3, 2025.

## Products and regulatory status

- Nevro's FY2024 10-K says Senza launched commercially in the United States in May 2015, and has been available in certain European markets since November 2010 and in Australia since August 2011.
- The 10-K describes the company's proprietary paresthesia-free "10 kHz Therapy" and cites its SENZA randomized controlled trial (company statements). The Senza HFX iQ is described there as the latest addition to the Senza family.

## Funding and ownership

- Globus Medical's 8-K reports the merger closed on April 3, 2025, with Nevro surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Globus. Nevro stock stopped trading on the NYSE that day.
