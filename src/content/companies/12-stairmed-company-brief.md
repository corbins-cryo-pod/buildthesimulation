StairMed is a Shanghai-based medical technology company reported to be running invasive BMI/BCI trials with *ultra-flexible electrodes* connected to a *coin-sized implant* embedded in the skull.

### Reported first-in-man trial (Shanghai / Huashan Hospital)

Shanghai’s English-language government news portal (citing chinadaily.com.cn) reports:
- implant at *Huashan Hospital (Fudan University)* on March 25, 2025
- two ultra-flexible electrodes ("~one-hundredth the thickness of human hair")
- coin-sized implant embedded in the skull, wireless transmission of signals
- after training, the participant could control games (chess, racing)
- article frames this as China’s *first invasive BMI first-in-man clinical trial*

- Shanghai.gov.cn page: <https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Latest-WhatsNew/20250513/f335be254e5c4dd5b00f3a26a04ab3cc.html>

Policy/ecosystem context (state roadmap):

### Why it’s interesting

StairMed is part of a broader Chinese push to build an invasive BCI pipeline (hardware + clinical deployment). The immediate technical questions are:
- signal quality and stability
- safety and migration
- how much “bandwidth” is achievable with very small electrode counts

### What to watch

1) *Peer-reviewed data / registry details*
2) *Indication focus* (assistive control vs rehab vs communication)
3) *Hardware evolution* (channels, packaging, recharge, longevity)

### 2026 update (reviewed Oct 4, 2026)

- *Funding (reported, Mar-Apr 2026):* StairMed announced an *RMB 500M* (about $73M) financing led by Alibaba, with Tencent among existing investors participating, and plans for larger registration trials. Sources: <https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/stairmed-secures-rmb-500-million-financing-led-by-alibaba-joined-by-tencent-302732525.html> and <https://kr-asia.com/alibaba-leads-rmb-500-million-investment-in-bci-startup-stairmed>
- *Funding update (Oct 28, 2026, company statement):* StairMed said it has raised about RMB 1.4B (about $192.5M) since H2 2026, not itemized by round, and RMB 2.6B (about $357.5M) in total as of September, which it describes as the most of any invasive BCI company in China. Named backers include Tencent, Alibaba, Geely Capital, CATL, OrbiMed, Qiming and Lilly Asia Ventures. The company also claims 42 long-term implants and says the WRS01 completed registration-trial enrollment in August. All figures are company-reported and unaudited; lead investors, round structure and valuation were not disclosed. Dealroom's Oct 30 headline says $210M but its body gives $192.5M, matching the company's RMB 1.4B figure, which is the one used here. Sources: <https://dealroom.co/news/168239-chinas-stairmed-raises-210m-for-brain-computer-implants-ranking-in-the-9/> and <https://insidebci.com/market/2026-10-29-neuroxess-rmb-700-million-series-b-sdic-juli-shenzhen-capital-registration-trial/>
- *Funding / listing preparation (Nov 2, 2026):* StairMed filed with the China Securities Regulatory Commission's Shanghai office for pre-listing tutoring - the formal preparation Chinese companies complete before applying for an A-share listing - with Huatai United Securities as adviser. The filing was reported by The Paper (Chinese) and Inside BCI. The same coverage clarifies the RMB 1.4B item above as money raised "since July" without named rounds or dates. Tutoring is not a listing application; no exchange or timetable has been disclosed.
