# Carnegie Mellon University, Yu and Chase groups

The 2014 Nature paper lists Byron M. Yu in the Departments of Electrical and Computer Engineering and Biomedical Engineering, and Steven M. Chase in Biomedical Engineering, at Carnegie Mellon University, with the Center for the Neural Basis of Cognition in Pittsburgh. Yu and Aaron Batista (University of Pittsburgh) contributed equally as senior authors.

## Cataloged hardware and applications

- [Neural constraints on learning, macaque BCI, 2014](/applications/158-bci-neural-constraints-on-learning-macaque-2014/): two animals with 96-channel Blackrock arrays in motor cortex. Device context: [Utah microelectrode array](/devices/01-utah-microelectrode-array/). Pittsburgh partner: [Rehab Neural Engineering Labs](/companies/38-pitt-rehab-neural-engineering-labs-lab-brief/).

## Program claims versus study evidence

This is an animal study. It reports no human participants and no clinical device.

Other CMU studies, current projects, lab size and funding beyond the paper's acknowledgements are not described here.
