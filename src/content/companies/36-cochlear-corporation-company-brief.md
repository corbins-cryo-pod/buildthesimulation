Cochlear Limited (Sydney, Australia) is a public cochlear implant maker.

## Products and regulatory status

- Cochlear's FY25 results (year ended June 30, 2025) say it launched the Nucleus Nexa System in June 2025, which it calls the first smart cochlear implant system with upgradeable firmware (company statement).
- Specifications and MRI labeling are on the linked device sheets.

## Financials (company-reported)

- FY25 sales revenue was A$2,356 million, up 4% (3% in constant currency). Cochlear implant revenue rose 9% in constant currency and acoustics 6%; services revenue declined.

## Not public or not verified

- Headcount and the revenue split between ABI and cochlear implants were not read here; the ABI is a small part of the business and is not reported separately.
