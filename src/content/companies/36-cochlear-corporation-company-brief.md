*Cochlear Corporation* (Sydney, Australia) is named in a peer-reviewed overview as the maker of the only FDA-approved auditory brainstem implant. The latest model it lists, the ABI541, has 21 platinum contacts on an 8.5 by 3.0 mm paddle and works with the Nucleus 6 sound processor.

- Company site: <https://www.cochlear.com/>
- Overview: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6438789/>
