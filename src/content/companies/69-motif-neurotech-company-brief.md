Motif Neurotech is a Houston-based startup developing a wireless, battery-free brain stimulator for mental illness. Its lead product in development is the DOT microstimulator, aimed first at treatment-resistant depression. DOT is not an approved device; see the linked sheet for what has and has not been shown.

## Origins

- The company was formed through Rice University's Biotech Launch Pad around magnetoelectric wireless power transfer developed at Rice. Its Series A release names Rice faculty Jacob Robinson (CEO and founder) and Kaiyuan Yang, and physicians Sunil Sheth and Sameer Sheth, as founders.

## Funding and ownership

- Series A of $18.75 million, announced January 24, 2024, led by Arboretum Ventures, with KdT Ventures, Satori Neuro, Dolby Family Ventures, re.Mind Capital and Max Hodak joining, and existing investors Divergent Capital, TMC Venture Fund, PsyMed Ventures, Empath Ventures and Capital Factory. Proceeds were for developing DOT.

- Not public or not found: any FDA submission for DOT, the current headcount, and funding after the Series A.
