# UMC Utrecht, Ramsey group

The 2016 NEJM paper lists Mariska J. Vansteensel as first author and Nick F. Ramsey as last author. The study was approved by the ethics committee of the University Medical Center Utrecht. The paper's funding statement names the Government of the Netherlands and the European Union.

## Cataloged hardware and applications

- [Fully implanted ECoG BCI in locked-in ALS, 2016](/applications/153-utrecht-fully-implanted-ecog-bci-als-2016/): subdural strips, a chest-implanted transmitter and home typing in one participant.

## Program claims versus study evidence

The sheet reports the paper's numbers for one participant only. The implant combined Medtronic components certified for other uses; the paper says Medtronic supplied them free of charge to UMC Utrecht.

## Not public or not verified

Department names, current studies, group size and later participants are not stated in the source used here.
