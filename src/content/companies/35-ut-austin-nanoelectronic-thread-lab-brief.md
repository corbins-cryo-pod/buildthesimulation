The *University of Texas at Austin* Department of Biomedical Engineering published a news item on the 2017 *Science Advances* paper, "Ultraflexible nanoelectronic probes form reliable, glial scar-free neural integration."

- UT BME news: <https://bme.utexas.edu/news/new-ultra-flexible-probes-form-reliable-scar-free-integration-with-the-brain/>
- Paper (PubMed): <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28246640/>

### Device entries

- [Nanoelectronic thread probes (NET)](/devices/27-nanoelectronic-thread-net-probes/): subcellular-dimension ultraflexible electrodes that the authors report integrate without a glial scar and track single units for months. Preclinical.
