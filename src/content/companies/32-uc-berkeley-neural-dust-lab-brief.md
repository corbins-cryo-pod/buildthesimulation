The neural dust work comes from the *University of California, Berkeley*. The 2016 Neuron paper, "Wireless Recording in the Peripheral Nervous System with Ultrasonic Neural Dust," lists Seo, Neely, Shen and others as authors. Berkeley News described the result as a step toward "electroceuticals."

- Berkeley News: <https://news.berkeley.edu/2016/08/03/sprinkling-of-neural-dust-opens-door-to-electroceuticals/>

### Device entries

- [Neural dust, ultrasonic backscatter mote](/devices/22-neural-dust-ultrasonic-backscatter-mote/): 3 mm mote with a piezoelectric crystal, one transistor and recording electrodes. Rat sciatic nerve and muscle. Berkeley's release reports a later shrink to a 1 mm cube.

### Limits

No single lab or principal investigator is named here because the sources read did not tie the project to one. Preclinical.
