# Wyss Center for Bio and Neuroengineering

The Wyss Center for Bio and Neuroengineering, Geneva, is listed as an author affiliation on the 2022 Nature Communications study (authors including Ioannis Vlachos, Jonas Zimmermann, Arnau Espinosa and Alessandro Tonin per the paper's record). The corresponding author email in the record is at wysscenter.ch.

## Cataloged hardware and applications

- [Intracortical speller in completely locked-in ALS, 2022](/applications/154-completely-locked-in-als-intracortical-speller-2022/): joint work with the [University of Tübingen group](/companies/80-university-of-tubingen-birbaumer-group/). Device context: [Utah microelectrode array](/devices/01-utah-microelectrode-array/).

## Program claims versus study evidence

This brief covers the center's role in one cataloged paper. Other Wyss Center programs are not in the catalog and are not described here.
