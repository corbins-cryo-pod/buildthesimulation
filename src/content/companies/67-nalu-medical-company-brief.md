Nalu Medical makes the Nalu Neurostimulation System, a small implanted peripheral nerve stimulator that is powered through an external therapy disc worn over the implant. The company was based in Carlsbad, California. Boston Scientific completed its acquisition of Nalu Medical in January 2026, so Nalu is no longer an independent private company.

## Funding and ownership

- Nalu closed an $85 million Series E in March 2024: $65 million led by Novo Holdings, plus an incremental $20 million from B Capital (company release).
- Boston Scientific says it announced a deal to acquire Nalu in October 2025 and completed it by its fourth-quarter 2025 results release. Trade press (MassDevice) reports an upfront cash payment of about $533 million for the remaining equity, or $600 million counting Boston Scientific's existing stake, and that Boston Scientific had been a strategic investor since 2017. Those deal figures are press-reported, not taken from a Boston Scientific filing read here.

## Products and regulatory status
