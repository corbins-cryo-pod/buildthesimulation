# MED-EL cochlear implant system

The MED-EL Cochlear Implant System is an FDA-approved cochlear implant family. This sheet uses the FDA summary for supplement S129 (approved October 3, 2024) and MED-EL's SYNCHRONY 2 page. It is thin on hardware: electrode geometry and implant dimensions are left blank.

Company brief: [MED-EL](/companies/65-med-el-company-brief/).

## Geometry and architecture

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Interface type | A variant of an active electrode array in the cochlea; the SSED notes hearing preservation claims for the FLEX electrode series [1] |
| Array layout | SYNCHRONY 2 (Mi1250) arrays are straight and flexible; 12 channels, with FLEX34 laid out as 5 single contacts at the apical end and 7 contact pairs at the base, 2.6 mm between channels [4] |
| Electrode count | Varies by array: FLEX34, FLEX28 and FLEX24 variants list 19 platinum contacts; arrays listed with 24 platinum contacts include a 26.4 mm, a 20.9 mm and a 12.1 mm stimulation range. 12 independent electrode channels, driven by 24 independent current sources [4] |
| Pitch |  |
| Electrode lengths | FLEX34 is 34 mm long (28.6 mm stimulation range); FLEX28 is 28 mm and FLEX24 is 24 mm long per the MED-EL surgical guideline. Other arrays list stimulation ranges of 26.4, 20.9, 15.4 and 12.1 mm [4] |
| Shank width and thickness | Basal end diameter 1.3 mm and FLEX-Tip apical end 0.5 x 0.4 mm on the FLEX34 array; the 24-contact 20.9 mm array lists 0.8 mm basal and 0.5 mm apical diameter [4] |
| Tip and exposed site geometry |  |
| Contact coating |  |
| Insulation | Medical grade silicone carrier; wires are platinum/iridium (90/10). Parylene C is listed among the materials in direct tissue contact [4] |
| Insertion method | Array inserted through a cochleostomy or round window opening; FLEX-Tip design is stated by MED-EL to reduce insertion force. A marker ring on the lead (for FLEX34, 34 mm from the tip) indicates the deepest insertion [4] |
| Anchoring and fixation | Stimulator housing seated in a bone bed; the PIN variant adds fixation pins for stability (manufacturer claim). Coil holds the external processor with a removable implant magnet [5] |

## Clinical and preclinical evidence

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Human subjects | IDE G170111: 44 participants in the database through March 27, 2023; enrolled April 26, 2018 to March 27, 2023 [1] |
| Preclinical cohort |  |
| Follow-up duration | 6 months after activation for the primary endpoints [1] |
| Indications | Adults 18 and older with bilateral moderate to profound sensorineural hearing loss and limited benefit from appropriately fit hearing aids (CNC 50% or less in the implant ear) [1] |

## Version boundary

SYNCHRONY 2 (Mi1250) is the newest implant in the S129 component list alongside SONATA 2, SYNCHRONY ST, SYNCHRONY, CONCERTO 2 and SONATATI100. Processors SONNET 2 and RONDO 3 are the newest listed.
