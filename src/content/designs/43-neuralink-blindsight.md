# Neuralink Blindsight

Blindsight is Neuralink's visual prosthesis, meant per Neuralink and Elon Musk's statement quoted by Reuters to let even people who have lost both eyes and their optic nerve see. The FDA breakthrough device designation is confirmed; whether anyone has yet seen anything through it is not confirmed by a primary source. A third-party site reported on September 30, 2026 that a participant perceived points of light; no Neuralink or Reuters source was found, so this sheet does not treat it as established and lists the status as preclinical. Compare the earlier cortical visual prosthesis in this catalog, the [Dobelle implant](/devices/34-dobelle-cortical-visual-prosthesis/).
