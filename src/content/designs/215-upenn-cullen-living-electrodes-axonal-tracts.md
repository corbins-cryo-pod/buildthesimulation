# UPenn engineered axonal tract living electrodes

The Cullen Lab at the University of Pennsylvania develops biohybrid interfaces in which engineered axonal tracts serve as "living electrodes": living biological conduits that interface with neural circuitry through synaptic mechanisms rather than metal contacts alone. The representative paper demonstrates synaptic-based modulation of neural circuitry with these constructs.
