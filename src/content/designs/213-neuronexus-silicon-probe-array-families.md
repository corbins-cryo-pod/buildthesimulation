# NeuroNexus silicon probe and array families

NeuroNexus Technologies (Ann Arbor, founded 2004) supplies configurable microelectrode probes and arrays for extracellular recording and stimulation, plus supporting electrophysiology systems and accessories. It is an infrastructure-layer supplier: laboratories and translational teams use its hardware rather than NeuroNexus selling an end-user BCI product.
