# Tsinghua SIGS CHIP all-organic 128-channel hydrogel ECoG

An electrode array with no metal in the recording path. The conductive layer is CHIP, a hydrogel made by letting PSS chains diffuse into a polyacrylamide-alginate network while PEDOT collects at the surface as a connected network. It comes from Xu Xiaomin's group at Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School with collaborators. Paper: "An exceptionally conductive hydrogel for all-organic, ultraflexible, and chronic neural interfaces", PNAS 2026, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2532840123 (open access, PMC13142910). The Chinese release is SIGS, 12 May 2026.

| Regulatory status | Research device [2] |

## Geometry and architecture

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Channels | 128, quad-layer structure [2] |
| Thickness | 9 um total [1][2] |
| Density and pitch | 853 channels per cm2 at a 40 um pitch; authors state this pitch was chosen for reliability and 20 um is reachable [2] |
| Linewidth | 40 um for sites and interconnects; 20 um linewidth and spacing shown with aerosol printing [2] |

## Materials and fabrication

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Conductor | CHIP: PEDOT:PSS percolated into PAAm-alginate hydrogel; percolation layer about 2 um thick; in-plane conductivity up to 2512 S/cm at high water content [1][2] |
| Patterning | Aerosol printing; sub-10 um said to be attainable by electrohydrodynamic printing or photolithographic dry etching [2] |
| Swelling control | Pre-swelling of the hydrogel and hydrophilic modification of the substrate suppress in-plane swelling so spacing stays fixed [1][2] |
| Insulation | Ultrathin Parylene film deposited by CVD over the conductive network [2] |
| Stack lamination | Cold-laminated to under 10 um [2] |

## Performance envelope

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Impedance | Tissue-electrode impedance 8.2 kOhm at 1 kHz [2]. CHIP stable in PBS for over 100 days, with a 4% rise in the first 6 days [2] |
| Mechanics | Elongation at break about 124% [1] |
| Acute recording | Rat, 128 channels across several areas; amplitude, SNR and spectra better than platinum electrodes per the release [1] |
| Chronic recording | Up to 550 days in freely moving rabbit; no clear inflammation or tissue damage reported [1][2] |

## Limits

The 128-channel array is a first demonstration at a conservative pitch. No human or primate data. Quad-layer composition is not itemized in the text read here.
