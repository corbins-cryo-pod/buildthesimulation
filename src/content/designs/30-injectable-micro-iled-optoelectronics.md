# Injectable micro-LED optoelectronics (wireless optogenetics)

An injectable class of cellular-scale semiconductor devices for wireless optogenetics. The building block is a GaN micro-inorganic LED (µ-ILED); multilayer probes combine µ-ILEDs with sensors and actuators, including a platinum microelectrode for recording or electrical stimulation. Optogenetics usually needs a fiber to the brain; this work places light sources at precise deep locations without a tether, under a wireless link. Dependence on optogenetic expression in target cells is not available in humans today.
