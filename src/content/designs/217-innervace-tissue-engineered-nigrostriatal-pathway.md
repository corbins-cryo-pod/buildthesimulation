# Innervace tissue-engineered nigrostriatal pathway

Innervace, a regenerative medicine startup co-founded by Penn Medicine's Kacy Cullen and Douglas Smith, is developing what Penn Center for Innovation describes as the first implantable biofabricated neural pathway to restore damaged or diseased brain circuitry. Its lead program aims to reconstruct the lost nigrostriatal pathway in patients with Parkinson's disease, using dopaminergic-phenotype cells in combination with a biofabricated scaffold pathway. The company closed a $40 million Series A (announced 2022) led by Deerfield Management.
