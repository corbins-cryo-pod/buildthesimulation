| Field | Value and source scope |

| Interface type | Regenerative sieve between transected nerve ends |
| Array layout | 2 mm diameter porous region; nine transit zones around eight electrode sites |
| Electrode count | Eight Pt-Ir sites; four curved around central zone, four straight on alternating spokes |
| Pitch | No uniform pitch reported; central transit hole about 600 µm diameter and each zone about 0.285 mm² |
| Electrode lengths | Not applicable to shanks; two 3 mm silicone conduit segments attached to sieve |
| Shank width and thickness | Polyimide wafer thickness unreported in audited article; assembly elastomer encapsulation 1-2 mm is not wafer thickness |

| Contact coating | Pt-Ir metallized/platinized sites as described by article; terminology preserved |
| Insulation | Polyimide base, medical epoxy at connections and elastomer encapsulation; silicone nerve conduits |
| Insertion method | Transect sciatic nerve, secure stumps in conduit assembly with about 4 mm gap |
| Anchoring and fixation | Nerve stumps secured inside conduits; connectors placed subcutaneously for terminal re-exposure |

## Engineering tradeoffs

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Strengths | Large transit zones with regenerated axons and selective stimulation at three months |
| Limitations | Transection required; PCB stiffens thin sieve; no long-term human or recording evidence |
| Scaling constraints | Eight large sites access sectors rather than isolate each axon; density versus transit-area tradeoff |
