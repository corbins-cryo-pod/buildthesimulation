# Bridge Neurotech non-invasive fUSI BCI

Bridge Neurotech (Redwood City, founded 2026) is building a fully non-invasive BCI: functional ultrasound imaging (fUSI) of brain activity through the skull, decoded with AI models. The company emerged from stealth on Oct 6, 2026 with a $13.5M raise and a research and development agreement with Butterfly Network, licensing Butterfly's Ultrasound-on-Chip technology; the agreement includes commitments to purchase Butterfly hardware.
