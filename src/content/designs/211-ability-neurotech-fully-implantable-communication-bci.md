# ABILITY Neurotech fully implantable communication BCI

ABILITY Neurotech (Geneva, Campus Biotech) is developing a fully implantable communication BCI for people with severe paralysis. Per the company's study page, the implant captures neural activity through 4 electrode arrays on the brain surface and wirelessly transmits it for decoding into computer commands. Trade press reported the wireless link as optical.
