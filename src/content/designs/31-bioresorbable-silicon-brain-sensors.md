# Bioresorbable silicon brain sensors

Wireless pressure and temperature sensors built from bioresorbable silicon. The paper argues that permanent electronic hardware acts as a nidus for infection and forces retrieval surgery, and that a device that resorbs avoids both. The sensors do not record neurons; they are listed because transient, wireless, resorbable electronics are a direction for temporary brain interfaces.
