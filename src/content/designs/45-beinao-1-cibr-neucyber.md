# Beinao-1 (CIBR, NeuCyber)

Beinao-1 (also written Beinao No. 1, "The NeuCyber Matrix BCI System") is a second Chinese semi-invasive BCI program alongside [NEO](/devices/41-neo-neuracle-tsinghua-epidural-bci/). It was developed by the Chinese Institute for Brain Research, Beijing (CIBR) with its spin-out NeuCyber NeuroTech (Beijing). China is a secondary region in this catalog. Reported implant counts disagree between sources and are given as published, not reconciled. China Daily reports Beinao-2 (NeuCyber Array), an invasive system with a 512-channel fully implantable wireless device, has entered animal trials; that is a different system and is not covered by this sheet's values.
