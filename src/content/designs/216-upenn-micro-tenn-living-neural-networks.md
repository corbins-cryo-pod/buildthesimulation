# UPenn micro-TENN living neural networks

Micro-tissue engineered neural networks (micro-TENNs) are the Cullen Lab's implantable living micro-tissue constructs: discrete populations of neurons with long-projecting axons, built at microscale so they can be transplanted into the brain to reconstruct circuitry, modulate networks, or serve as biological relays. Distinct from the lab's [living electrode axon tracts](/devices/215-upenn-cullen-living-electrodes-axonal-tracts/), which are the interface-oriented subset of this platform.
