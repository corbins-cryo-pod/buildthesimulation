# TUM custom 256-microelectrode BCI implant

A team at Technical University of Munich (TUM) University Hospital implanted a custom BCI with 256 microelectrodes in a patient with quadriplegia, in a surgery lasting more than five hours. NeuroNews International reported it as the first procedure of this kind performed in Europe (their framing). The electrodes capture signals from an area involved in planning and executing grasp movements; the goal is smartphone control and eventually robotic-arm control, with ongoing twice-weekly sessions to train AI decoders.
