# Head-mounted wireless neurosensor (Brown, 2014)

> *One-line verdict:* Cut the tether for primate cortical recording: full-spectrum signals during natural locomotion and sleep, over a receiver network that the authors say scales to hundreds of channels and large environments.

*Quick tags:* Recording · Intracortical · Species: Nonhuman primate · Published: 2014 (Neuron)

---

### Overview

*What it is:* A compact, lightweight, low-power, head-mounted wireless neurosensor with a high data rate. It is described as electrically safe and compatible with most existing neural probes.

*What was shown:* Cortical neuron populations in freely behaving nonhuman primates during natural locomotion and sleep-wake transitions in ecologically equivalent settings.

*Link design:* A spatially distributed network of synchronized receivers, scalable to hundreds of channels and vast environments.

*Relation to the 2013 system:* The 2013 entry is a subcutaneous package for clinical-style use. This 2014 device is a head-mounted unit for free behavior.
