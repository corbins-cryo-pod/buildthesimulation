# Inspire upper airway stimulation

Inspire is an implanted hypoglossal nerve stimulator with a sensing lead that tracks breathing. This sheet uses the FDA overview for the June 2023 indication expansion and the supplement record for the Inspire V IPG (Model 3150, decision August 1, 2024). Lead and electrode specifications are not in those sources and stay Unreported.

## Clinical and preclinical evidence

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Human subjects | STAR pivotal trial: 126 implanted subjects at 22 sites; baseline mean age 54.5, 83% male, mean BMI 28.4, baseline AHI mean 32.0 (median 29.3, range 13.3 to 65.1) [4] |
| Preclinical cohort | Canine studies: 4 animals with bilateral lead implantation for 8 to 12 weeks, and 4 animals for the full system over 12 weeks [4] |
| Follow-up duration | 12-month single-arm study followed by a randomized therapy withdrawal study at 13 months [4] |
| Indications | Moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea (AHI 15 to 100), age 22 and older, unable to use or tolerate PAP or BiPAP, and without complete concentric collapse of the soft palate; S090 raised the AHI limit from 65 to 100 and the BMI limit from 32 to 40 [1] |
| Trials and registries | STAR (G080122 / S28), multi-center, prospective, pivotal, 2010; feasibility study G950075 [4] |
| Primary outcomes | Co-primary endpoints were at least a 50% responder rate for AHI and for oxygen desaturation index at 12 months; the SSED states both were achieved along with all secondary endpoints. In the withdrawal study the first 46 responders were randomized 1:1 (23 ON, 23 OFF) [4] |
| Key limitations | The FDA overview and supplement record carry no lead geometry, electrode count, battery or trial outcome data. The 2014 SSED was read for the original configuration; the Inspire V (Model 3150) was not described in it. The Inspire V soft launch was targeted for late 2024 with full launch in 2025 per the company [1][2][3] |

## Version boundary

Inspire V Model 3150 is the new version of the Model 3028 IPG. The FDA overview describes the system at the S090 approval, before Inspire V. Values drawn from reference [4] describe the original 2014 approval configuration, not Inspire V.
