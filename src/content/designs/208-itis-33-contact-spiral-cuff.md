# ITIS/Ljubljana 33-contact spiral cuff

This is a distinct prototype from the [CWRU/Ardiem self-sizing cuff](/devices/10-spiral-nerve-cuff-electrode-helical/). The photograph shows manufactured hardware, rather than a rendered model.

## Identity

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Developer | ITIS, University of Ljubljana and Institute of Metals and Technology; Rozman et al., 2018 |
| Function | Extraneural stimulation of peripheral nerve |
| Evidence | Isolated porcine vagus nerve testing; human use of this photographed prototype not established |
| Regulatory status | Research prototype; approval not established |

| Placement | Wrapped around a peripheral nerve; does not penetrate brain tissue |

## Evidence and limitations

The paper reports 33 electrodes, but one Methods sentence describes thirteen groups of three. This unresolved inconsistency prevents a verified contact-layout reconstruction. The publisher correction concerns the title only.

Chronic human performance, impedance, charge-injection limit, recording performance and approved stimulation settings are not established here. The study's weld-strength tests are not a clinical safety rating.
