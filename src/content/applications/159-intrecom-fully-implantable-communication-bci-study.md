# INTRECOM communication BCI study

The INTRECOM study evaluates [ABILITY Neurotech's fully implantable communication BCI](/devices/211-ability-neurotech-fully-implantable-communication-bci/) at UMC Utrecht, with Medical Research Ethics Committee approval. Per the company's study page:

- participants: ages 18 to 70 with severe paralysis and speech problems from traumatic brain injury, stroke, neurodegenerative or neuromuscular disease (including ALS); still able to communicate by other means such as eye blinking; cognitively unimpaired
- the study lasts about one year, with home-based training and a goal of independent home use
- clinical lead: Dr Mariska Vansteensel, Associate Professor at UMC Utrecht

Trade press reported on Oct 12-13, 2026 that the company had begun first-in-human testing; the company's own study page confirms recruiting but does not state an implant date, so that milestone is reported-only here.
