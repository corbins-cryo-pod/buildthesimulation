# Acute Argo recordings in rats and sheep

The 2021 [Argo hardware](/devices/94-argo-microwire-cmos-recording-system/) study tests two distinct electrode preparations. The authors' Paradromics affiliation grounds the company link, not a claim that these results came from today's Connexus clinical device.

## Rat spikes

The methods describe five 5-10 mm microwire arrays implanted into rat somatosensory and prefrontal cortex. The illustrated 1,300-wire array yielded 791 isolated single units under anesthesia. This is a reported array example, not 791 units from every array or a 65,536-neuron recording.

## Sheep surface auditory maps

Two sheep underwent acute surface recordings with 12 × 12 mm, 60 µm-pitch arrays. The electrodes recorded local field potentials, not isolated spikes from tens of thousands of neurons.

| Pink-noise analysis | Connected channels | Above response threshold | Statistically significant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One sheep | 30,146 | 21,963 (73%) | 16,412 (54% of total) |
| Second sheep | 31,239 | 17,233 (55%) | 12,590 (40% of total) |

The response threshold was at least 10 µV RMS above baseline. Threshold crossing and statistical significance are different counts. Pure-tone analysis found frequency-specific responses and emerging tonotopic organization; placement, animal variation and operating-room noise could affect the maps.

## Boundaries

These are acute, head-fixed, anesthetized preparations. They do not establish chronic survival, stable speech decoding, assistive-device control or a completed human implant result. The electronics' larger capacity and greater-than-eight-hour continuous-recording specification are not the implanted channel count or a long-term tissue follow-up.

The paper discusses a smaller floating architecture as future development. The modern Connexus study is cataloged separately rather than projected backward onto Argo.
