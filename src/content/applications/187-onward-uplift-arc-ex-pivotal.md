# ONWARD Up-LIFT pivotal trial (NCT04697472)

The completed pivotal trial of ONWARD's ARC-EX system - external electrical stimulation delivered over the cervical spinal cord during structured rehabilitation - for improving hand and arm function in people with chronic cervical spinal cord injury. Registry facts are from ClinicalTrials.gov (verified November 9, 2026). Results are peer reviewed; the trial was the basis for the FDA's De Novo marketing authorization of the ARC-EX System on December 19, 2024, the first US authorization of a non-invasive spinal cord stimulation system for chronic spinal cord injury.

## Study facts

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Registry | NCT04697472; interventional, single-arm, open-label, sequential design: functional task practice alone, then task practice plus ARCEX Therapy; non-significant risk [1] |
| Official title | The Up-LIFT Study of Non-Invasive ARC Therapy for Spinal Cord Injury [1] |
| Sponsor | ONWARD Medical, Inc. [1] |
| Status | Completed [1] |
| Dates | Actual start January 8, 2021; completion September 13, 2022 [1] |
| Enrollment | 65 (actual); 60 completed the protocol [1][2] |
| Population | Chronic tetraplegia due to cervical spinal cord injury [1][2] |
| Primary outcomes | Incidence of serious adverse events; number of participants with change in upper extremity strength and function compared with the end of an equivalent period of rehabilitation alone [1][2] |
| Registry results | No results posted to ClinicalTrials.gov as of November 9, 2026 [1] |

## Results (peer reviewed)

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Publication | Moritz, Field-Fote, Tefertiller et al., Nature Medicine 2024;30(5):1276-1283 (online May 20, 2024); open access [2] |
| Design reported | Prospective, single-arm, multicenter, open-label trial of ARCEX Therapy during structured rehabilitation [2] |
| Safety | No serious adverse events related to ARCEX Therapy were reported [2] |
| Primary effectiveness | Met: 72% of participants demonstrated improvements greater than the minimally important difference criteria for both strength and functional domains [2] |
| Secondary endpoints | Significant improvements in fingertip pinch force, hand prehension and strength, upper extremity motor and sensory abilities, and self-reported quality of life [2] |

## Regulatory outcome

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| FDA pathway | De Novo DEN240014, ARC-EX System, Onward Medical, Inc.; decision December 19, 2024 [3] |
