# Nurotron WH-01A ABI phase III trial in NF2 (ChiCTR2300070545)

The registered phase III trial behind the [Nurotron WH-01A auditory brainstem implant](/devices/190-nurotron-wh-01a-auditory-brainstem-implant/): a prospective, single-center, open-label, single-group, target-value study in patients with severe-or-worse deafness caused by neurofibromatosis type 2, at Shanghai Ninth People's Hospital. Registry facts are from ChiCTR (verified November 3, 2026). The trial preceded China's NMPA approval of the device in October 2026; approval and first-patient details are regulator, press and company reporting, labeled as such.

## Study facts (ChiCTR2300070545)

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Registry | ChiCTR2300070545, "Clinical Trail of Auditory Brainstem Implant System in NF2 Patient" (title as registered); interventional, phase 3, single arm, open label, target-value design, prospective registration [1] |
| Sponsor and leader | Shanghai Ninth People's Hospital, Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine; study leader Hao Wu; device maker named as Zhejiang Norkang Neuroelectronic Technology (Nurotron); ethics approval SH9H-2022-C61-3, November 22, 2022 [1] |
| Registration | Registered April 15, 2023; last refreshed June 30, 2023; execution period April 15, 2023 to December 31, 2026; recruiting window April 15, 2023 to April 30, 2025 [1] |
| Status caveat | The registry still shows "recruiting" but was last refreshed in June 2023 and its recruiting window closed April 30, 2025; the device has since been approved (October 2026), so the registry entry is stale [1][2] |
| Enrollment | 20 (experimental group) [1] |
| Population | Severe-or-worse deafness caused by neurofibromatosis type 2, including patients expected to be completely deaf after surgery; ages 6 to 75; expected postoperative life expectancy over 2 years [1] |
| Primary outcome | Total efficacy rate at 12 months after activation [1] |
| Secondary outcomes | Acoustic field threshold assessment (1, 3, 6, 12 months after activation); electrophysiological assessment (intraoperative through 12 months); electrode placement evaluation; adverse event rate [1] |

## Approval and context

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Approval | NMPA approved the WH-01A innovative-product registration in October 2026 (notice dated October 20; Sixth Tone dates the approval October 19), described by the regulator as China's first auditory brainstem implant [2][3] |
| First domestic implant | January 26, 2022, a 5-year-old with bilateral cochlear nerve hypoplasia and narrow internal auditory canals; switched on March 1, 2022 (company release, before this trial's registration) [4] |
| Regulatory path | NMPA innovative-device special review notice 2024 No. 3, published March 15, 2024, lists the project (per the company; the notice itself was not opened); special review is not approval [4] |
| Licensee discrepancy | The hospital named Shanghai Hansheng Medical Technology as licensee in 2021, while Nurotron says it co-developed and commercializes the device; the relationship is not stated in the sources read, so both are shown [4][5] |
| Evidence gap | No peer-reviewed outcome paper for the WH-01A identified as of November 3, 2026; the registry lists a target-value design without a results posting [1] |
