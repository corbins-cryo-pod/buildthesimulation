# Inspire STAR pivotal trial in obstructive sleep apnea (NCT01161420)

The pivotal trial behind the [Inspire upper-airway stimulation system](/devices/158-inspire-upper-airway-stimulation/), an implanted hypoglossal nerve stimulator for obstructive sleep apnea: a multicenter, prospective, single-group cohort published in the New England Journal of Medicine, with a randomized controlled therapy-withdrawal phase in participants who responded. Figures are from the NEJM abstract, the ClinicalTrials.gov record and the FDA summary of safety and effectiveness, verified November 7, 2026. Company brief: [Inspire Medical Systems](/companies/58-inspire-medical-systems-company-brief/).

## STAR pivotal cohort

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Registry | NCT01161420, "Effects of the Inspire Implantable Nerve Stimulation System on Obstructive Sleep Apnea"; sponsor Inspire Medical Systems; status completed; start July 2010, primary completion March 2013, completion April 2017; interventional, single-group, no masking; registry enrollment 929 actual across the full study record including follow-up phases [3] |
| Design | Multicenter, prospective, single-group cohort at 22 sites; device surgically implanted in patients with moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea who had difficulty accepting or adhering to CPAP; co-primary endpoints AHI and ODI responder rates at 12 months [1][2] |
| Pivotal cohort | 126 participants; 83% men; mean age 54.5 years; mean BMI 28.4 [1] |
| 12-month result | Median AHI decreased 68%, from 29.3 to 9.0 events per hour (P < 0.001); median ODI decreased 70%, from 25.4 to 7.4 events per hour (P < 0.001); secondary measures (Epworth Sleepiness Scale, FOSQ) showed reduced sleep-apnea effects and improved quality of life [1] |
| Withdrawal phase | Consecutive responders randomized to therapy maintenance (n=23) or withdrawal (n=23); maintenance group mean AHI stayed at the 12-month level (8.9 versus 7.2 events per hour, not significant) while the withdrawal group worsened significantly (25.8 versus 7.6, P < 0.001); ODI followed the same pattern [1] |
| Safety | Procedure-related serious adverse event rate below 2% [1] |
| Evidence caveat | The authors describe the study as an uncontrolled cohort; the randomized phase demonstrates the effect reverses without stimulation but the primary analysis has no parallel control group [1] |
| Funding | Funded by Inspire Medical Systems [1] |

## Regulatory outcome

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Approval | FDA premarket approval P130008 on April 30, 2014, on the strength of the STAR cohort [2] |
| Longer follow-up | The FDA summary records 680 adverse events through 18 months in 117 of 126 subjects (93%), most of them non-serious; later model changes (Inspire IV, Inspire V) are outside this trial's scope [2] |
