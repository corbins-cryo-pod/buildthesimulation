# StairMed WRS early human trials (NCT06829212, NCT06944834, NCT07647315)

The three registered early human trials of StairMed's [WRS implantable wireless BCI](/devices/178-stairmed-wrs-ultraflexible-wireless-bci/), which pairs ultra-flexible penetrating electrodes with a battery-free skull-mounted implant: two motor studies in paralysis and amputation and one Mandarin speech neuroprosthesis study. Registry facts are from ClinicalTrials.gov (verified October 30, 2026). Published human data so far are intraoperative only; chronic implant progress figures are company disclosures and are labeled as such.

## Motor decoding trial facts (NCT06829212)

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Registry | NCT06829212; prospective, interventional, single-group, no masking [1] |
| Sponsor and site | Shanghai StairMed Technology Co., Ltd., with Huashan Hospital, Fudan University (Shanghai); 1 site [1] |
| Status and dates | Recruiting; estimated start March 2025; estimated primary completion and completion February 2027 [1] |
| Enrollment | 4 (estimated) [1] |
| Population | Spinal cord injury, brainstem stroke, ALS or other motor neuron diseases causing partial or complete paralysis, or bilateral upper-limb amputation; diagnosis stable at least 6 months, condition present at least 1 year; ages 18 to 80 [1] |
| Primary outcome | Device-related adverse events through study completion, an average of 7 months [1] |
| Secondary outcomes | Adverse events and serious adverse events; information throughput in bits per second from cursor tasks; monthly usage hours; task accuracy; psychological state; caregiver burden [1] |

## Motor rehabilitation trial facts (NCT06944834)

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Registry | NCT06944834; interventional, single-group, no masking, device feasibility [2] |
| Sponsor and site | StairMed; the Second Affiliated Hospital of Air Force Medical University (Xi'an); 1 site [2] |
| Status and dates | Not yet recruiting as of October 30, 2026; estimated start April 2025; estimated completion April 2027 [2] |
| Enrollment | 5 (estimated) [2] |
| Population | Complete or incomplete quadriplegia from spinal cord injury, brainstem stroke, ALS or other motor neuron diseases, or bilateral upper-limb amputation; upper-limb muscle strength at or below grade 3; ages 18 to 65 [2] |
| Primary outcome | Incidence of device- or procedure-related adverse events within 6 months of implantation [2] |
| Secondary outcomes | Cursor-control accuracy, path efficiency and time consumption; ISNCSCI neurological scores; SF-36 quality of life; MoCA cognitive scores; PHQ-9, HAMA and HAMD psychological scores [2] |

## Mandarin speech neuroprosthesis trial facts (NCT07647315)

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Registry | NCT07647315; prospective, single-arm, no masking, device feasibility [3] |
| Sponsor and site | StairMed; Huashan Hospital, Fudan University (Shanghai); 1 site [3] |
| Status and dates | Recruiting; actual start September 1, 2026; estimated primary completion August 30, 2028; estimated completion December 30, 2028 [3] |
| Enrollment | 4 (estimated) [3] |
| Population | Speech disorders including locked-in syndrome, severe dysarthria, aphasia with preserved auditory comprehension, or late-stage ALS-related language dysfunction, secondary to stroke, traumatic brain injury, ALS or other motor neuron diseases; ages 18 to 80 [3] |
| Primary outcome | Incidence of device-related adverse events from implantation to month 12 [3] |
| Secondary outcomes | All adverse events; device deficiencies; number of reliable recording channels at 3, 6, 9 and 12 months; speech output rate in Chinese characters per minute; speech output character error rate [3] |

## Evidence behind the trials

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Peer-reviewed human data | Intraoperative only: 719 single neurons isolated from 1,302 valid channels across 11 of 16 patients, maximum 135 neurons simultaneously in one patient, recordings up to 36.5 minutes, Nature Communications 2026; the arrays used were research versions, not the chronic implant [4] |
| Company-reported implant progress | First chronic wireless implant March 25, 2025 in an amputee patient at Huashan; 18 cumulative implants disclosed at July 20, 2026; cursor control within 2 to 3 weeks of training (company disclosures via trade and secondary press, not audited or peer reviewed) [5][6] |
| Regulatory context | The WRS01 system entered China's NMPA innovative-device special review in November 2025, reported as the first invasive BCI to do so; special review is not approval, and company statements describe a larger registration trial planned for 2026 onward (Chinese-language press and company statements) [7] |
| Evidence gap | No peer-reviewed chronic safety or performance data for the implant; channel count, sampling rate and impedance of the chronic implant are unpublished [1][4] |
