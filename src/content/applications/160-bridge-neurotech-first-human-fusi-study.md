# Bridge Neurotech first human fUSI study

Bridge Neurotech's first human research study is enrolling in Redwood City, per the founder's letter and Butterfly Network's press release:

- adults who have had a cranioplasty with a PEEK or Longeviti implant
- healthy adults 18 and older

No decoding results in humans have been published yet; whether the study produces published human decoding results is the company's own named milestone.
