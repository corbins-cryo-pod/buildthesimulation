# CereGate freezing-of-gait clinical investigation

CereGate (Munich) is a clinical-stage neurotechnology company whose approach is software, not new hardware: its therapy software runs on existing Boston Scientific deep brain stimulation (DBS) systems and is currently in clinical investigation.

- first clinical indication: freezing of gait (FoG), a sudden interruption of walking that persists in some Parkinson's patients despite optimized medication and responds poorly to established DBS
- separately, the company conducts research in spinal cord stimulation

Clinical readouts have not been published in the sources used here.
