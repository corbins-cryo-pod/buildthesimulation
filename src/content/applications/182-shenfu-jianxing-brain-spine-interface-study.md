# Shenfu Jianxing brain-spine interface study (ChiCTR2500095359)

The registered study behind the [Shenfu Jianxing brain-spine interface](/devices/182-shenfu-jianxing-brain-spine-interface-fudan/): a single-arm investigator-initiated study at Zhongshan Hospital, Fudan University, testing brain-controlled closed-loop epidural electrical stimulation in paraplegia after spinal cord injury. Registry facts are from ChiCTR (verified November 2, 2026). Patient progress figures come from hospital and university releases, not a peer-reviewed paper, and are labeled as such.

## Study facts (ChiCTR2500095359)

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Registry | ChiCTR2500095359, "Study on the safety and effectiveness of brain controlled closed-loop epidural electrical stimulation for patients with lower limb paralysis after spinal cord injury"; interventional, single arm, no blinding, prospective registration [1] |
| Sponsor and leader | Zhongshan Hospital affiliated to Fudan University; study leader Jing Ding; ethics approval B2024-536R, December 31, 2024; funded by hospital development funds [1] |
| Registration | Registered and last refreshed January 6, 2025; execution period January 1 to December 31, 2025 [1] |
| Registry status caveat | The registry still shows "not yet recruiting," last refreshed at registration, while hospital releases report four patients implanted by March 3, 2025; the registry entry is stale and the execution window has passed [1][2] |
| Enrollment | 3 per the registry; the hospital release counts 4 patients including a Huashan Hospital case, and both figures are listed [1][2] |
| Population | Paraplegia after spinal cord injury, 6 months or more post-injury, injury level T12 or above, ASIA grade B, C or D, normal upper-limb motor function, unable to stand or walk independently; ages 18 to 65 [1] |
| Primary outcomes | ASIA score; lower-limb iliopsoas and quadriceps muscle strength; gravity-assist ratio required for standing [1] |
| Secondary outcomes | EMG amplitude of the iliopsoas and quadriceps during active movement; standing ability and standing time; sensory evoked potential amplitude and latency [1] |

## Reported patient progress

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Implants | First patient implanted January 8, 2025 at Zhongshan Hospital (a 34-year-old man with two years of paraplegia after a thoracic spinal injury); second and third in February 2025; fourth March 3, 2025 at Huashan Hospital (hospital release) [2] |
| First-patient course | Raised a leg on day 1, controlled both legs by brain on day 3, tried stepping on a ceiling rail on day 10, walked more than 5 m in a standing frame under suspension on day 15 and walked with a walker under suspension on day 49 (hospital release, not peer reviewed) [2] |
| Regulatory direction | Fudan reported in December 2025 that the product entered the US FDA Breakthrough Therapy pathway (university statement; the designation itself was not verified here); the team said in March 2025 it planned third-party type testing and registration-trial preparation [3][2] |
| Evidence gap | No peer-reviewed clinical paper identified as of November 2, 2026; hardware specifications (electrode counts, geometry) are unpublished [2] |
