# Motif XCS early feasibility study in TRD (NCT07594483)

The first registered human study of Motif Neurotech's eXternally powered Cortical Stimulation (XCS) System, built on the [DOT magnetoelectric stimulator](/devices/175-motif-dot-magnetoelectric-brain-stimulator/): a prospective, multi-center, single-arm, open-label early feasibility study in treatment-resistant depression. Registry facts are from ClinicalTrials.gov (verified October 30, 2026). The device evidence behind it is a peer-reviewed prototype paper and company-reported acute human demonstrations, labeled as such.

## Study facts (NCT07594483)

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Registry | NCT07594483; official title "Regulated Stimulation for Optimized Network Activity and Therapeutic Equilibrium"; interventional, single-group, no masking, primary purpose treatment [1] |
| Sponsor | Motif Neurotech, Inc., with collaborator Bright Research Partners [1] |
| Status | Recruiting [1] |
| Dates | Estimated start September 2026; estimated primary completion and completion December 2027 [1] |
| Enrollment | 20 (estimated) [1] |
| Sites | 3 US sites: Emory Healthcare (Atlanta), University of Iowa (Iowa City), Brain Health Consultants and TMS Center (Houston) [1] |
| Population | Unipolar non-psychotic major depressive disorder in a current major depressive episode; screening MADRS score of 20 or more; no sustained response to 2 or more adequately dosed antidepressants from at least 2 classes; ages 22 to 85 [1] |
| Primary outcome | Incidence of device- and/or procedure-related adverse events, from the procedure through 12 months of follow-up [1] |
| Secondary outcomes | Change in MADRS depression score (0 to 60, higher is worse) and incidence of stimulation-induced side effects, after treatment through 12 months [1] |

## Evidence behind the study

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Acute human demonstration | Intraoperative stimulation of the motor cortex with the prototype produced a hand contraction in one patient and thumb movement with EMG responses through the dura above 10 V in a second, during tumor resection surgery at Baylor St Luke's; first-in-human use reported September 13, 2023 (company release and peer-reviewed paper) [2][3] |
| Preclinical chronic data | 8 pigs, 10 implants, motor responses over chronic experiments in 3 animals implanted 20 to 35 days; saline bench testing held 250 to 400 ohm at 1 kHz over 22 days and more than 22 million pulses (peer reviewed) [3] |
| Version boundary | Hardware values come from the 2023 research prototype (about 9 mm, battery-free, two disc electrodes); whether the XCS clinical system matches that prototype is not stated in the sources read [3] |
| Evidence gap | No peer-reviewed human depression data for the XCS System identified as of October 30, 2026; the study's efficacy signal (MADRS change) is unproven until this trial reads out [1] |
