# MEG imagined and spoken phrase decoding (UF iBRAIN)

The UF Neurology iBRAIN Lab works on non-invasive BCI decoding with MEG and deep learning. The representative published study in this thread:

- Dash D, et al. *Decoding imagined and spoken phrases from non-invasive MEG signals.* Front Neurosci. 2020.

UF Neurology publicly describes BCI work aimed at synthesizing speech from neural activity using MEG and deep learning. This is a research thread, not a device program: no unique hardware is involved beyond standard MEG instrumentation.
