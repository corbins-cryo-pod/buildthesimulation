# Optogenetic living electrodes BMI study (2020)

A second major study for the Cullen Lab's [living electrode constructs](/devices/215-upenn-cullen-living-electrodes-axonal-tracts/):

- *Development of optically controlled "living electrodes" with long-projecting axon tracts for a synaptic brain-machine interface.* Science Advances, 2020.

The constructs carry optogenetically actuatable neurons so light drives synaptic output into host circuitry - a biological input channel for a brain-machine interface. Preclinical; human performance is not established by this source.
