# Nankai / Rongyuan interventional BCI first-in-human study (ChiCTR2400093125)

The registered study behind the [Nankai / Rongyuan interventional (stent-electrode) BCI](/devices/189-nankai-rongyuan-interventional-bci/): a clinical safety study of an intravenous interventional brain-machine sensor for paralysed limbs after cerebral infarction, at Fujian Sanbo Funeng Brain Hospital. Registry facts are from ChiCTR (verified November 2, 2026). The functional outcome (a patient regaining grasping) is institutional reporting, not a peer-reviewed paper, and is labeled as such.

## Study facts (ChiCTR2400093125)

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Registry | ChiCTR2400093125, "Clinical safety study of intravenous interventional brain-machine type A sensors for the treatment of paralysed limbs"; interventional, non-randomized control design, no blinding, prospective registration [1] |
| Sponsor and leader | Fujian Sanbo Funeng Brain Hospital (Capital Medical University), study leader Zhixiong Lin; ethics approval FJSBNK-YJ-2024-005-01, October 8, 2024; funded by Tianjin Rongyuan Intelligent Technology [1] |
| Registration | Registered and last refreshed November 28, 2024; execution period November 1, 2024 to May 31, 2025 [1] |
| Registry status caveat | The registry still shows "not yet recruiting," last refreshed at registration, while Nankai University reporting describes a first human implant in 2025; the registry entry is stale and the execution window has passed [1][2] |
| Enrollment | Test group sample size 1, plus a control group on routine rehabilitation [1] |
| Population | Limb motor dysfunction after cerebral infarction, disease duration within 6 months, ages 45 to 75; patent jugular veins and transverse sinuses confirmed by MRV or CTV, and functional activation in motor cortex near the superior sagittal sinus confirmed by fMRI [1] |
| Primary outcome | Electroencephalogram (EEG) [1] |

## Reported outcome and context

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| First human case | 67-year-old man with left-limb paralysis for six months after cerebral infarction; stent-electrode array placed on the intracranial vessel wall plus a subcutaneous wireless unit; no infection or thrombosis reported; combined with functional electrical stimulation in a closed loop; the patient regained grasping (Nankai University news, institutional reporting) [2] |
| Date uncertainty | The English Nankai page is dated July 3, 2025 on the page and September 2025 in its URL; the Nankai District government page is dated August 2025. The paper trail disagrees, so no single implant date is asserted [2][3] |
| Preclinical trail | Sheep trial June 25, 2022; non-human primate (macaque, jugular access, robotic-arm control) May 4, 2023; endovascular sensor retrieval in a sheep August 2024 (institutional and newspaper reporting; one peer-reviewed abstract) [2][4] |
| Evidence gap | No peer-reviewed paper describing the human device's electrode geometry, materials or channel count identified as of November 2, 2026 [2] |
