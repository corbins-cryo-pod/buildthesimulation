# Mindtrix cortical visual reconstruction first human study (2025)

The first reported human study for Mindtrix's [cortical visual reconstruction system](/devices/183-mindtrix-cortical-visual-reconstruction-system/), announced in December 2025. Sources are Chinese-language press and the company; no registry record or peer-reviewed paper was found, so claims are labeled by source.

## Study facts

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Study type | Investigator-initiated trial (IIT), per the founder's statement to China Science Daily [1] |
| Participant | One patient with occipital lobe epilepsy who already had temporary therapeutic electrodes placed for surgery; the stimulation study ran in the window after that implant. The patient was not fully blind and had a largely intact visual pathway [1] |
| Hardware used | Temporary clinical electrodes from the epilepsy workup, not the company's own implant; whether the company's ECOG-RS array was involved is not stated [1] |
| First reported result | The patient reported colored points and simple shapes when the visual cortex was stimulated with the eyes covered (China Science Daily, December 22, 2025) [1]. The company's announcement of a "global first" quantitative perception of complex shapes and colors was reported by Science and Technology Daily on December 5, 2025 [3] |
| Second reported result | In February 2026 the company announced a "world's first" grayscale visual reconstruction: subjects identified numbers and simple geometric shapes (company statement carried by VCBeat) [5] |
| Team | Founder Liu Bing, associate researcher at the Chinese Academy of Sciences Institute of Automation; company founded 2024 [1] |
| Status and plans | IIT stage. The founder said the team plans registration type testing in 2026 and a registration trial for blind patients in 2028 [1]. VCBeat reported (May 31, 2026) a planned IIT in totally blind patients [6]; chief marketing officer Chen Wenkai gave 2026 goals of 4 to 8 fully blind IIT participants and a market target around 2030 to 2031 [7][8]. No regulatory approval |
| Registry or paper | None found in ClinicalTrials.gov (searched October 25, 2026) or peer-reviewed indexes; no ChiCTR record surfaced in search |

## Limits

All human-result claims are company statements carried by Chinese press; the patient count is one, the hardware was not the product implant, and no data tables are published. Product claims for the intended system (glasses, VPU, RV-IPG stimulator, cortical array) stay on the device sheet.
