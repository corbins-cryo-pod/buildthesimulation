# Beinao-1 GCP registration trial (2026)

The registered multi-center trial of [Beinao-1](/devices/45-beinao-1-cibr-neucyber/), the wireless semi-invasive BCI from CIBR and NeuCyber. Facts are as published by the cited sources. Implant counts from the earlier investigator-initiated phase disagree between sources; those counts stay on the device sheet.

## Trial facts

| Field | Value and source scope |
| --- | --- |
| Registered status | Entered registered clinical trials in 2026 [1, 4] |
| Trial start | March 31, 2026, with the first two trial implants the same day at Tiantan Hospital and Xuanwu Hospital [1, 2, 3] |
| Design | GCP-compliant, multi-center [1] |
| Initial participants | Two spinal cord injury patients [1] |
| Registration indication | High paraplegia (tetraplegia) per Beijing News [2] |
| Planned enrollment | 36 patients initially [1, 3]; NeuCyber told Reuters it hopes to expand to 50 (company statement) [5] |
| Follow-up | 26 weeks per Xuanwu Hospital [3] |
| Registry identifier | No ChiCTR or ClinicalTrials.gov identifier found in the sources used here |
| Reported timeline | CIBR's director: 30 to 40 patients within 2026 and NMPA registration filing in 2027 (institute statement) [2]; NeuCyber: commercial availability two to three years out (company statement) [5] |
| Safety | Xuanwu Hospital reports no serious adverse events [3]; no adverse-event dataset published |
