      Section 03 / Inert electrical logic

      Which pads can still identify a tip?

      The fixture traces Boolean paths on 100 symbolic roots and checks 96 virtual outputs. Four corner roots are parked; they still exist in the graph. An open loses the intended output. A bridge can propagate a tag to another output.

      Reading the drawing

      Where the model stops

      In the verified historical cross-section the trench fill is glass. This drawing does not assert that current commercial arrays use a particular glass, ceramic, process stack or finished channel map. The measured quantities are geometric, not dielectric strength, fracture risk, leakage or recording quality.

      Every inspected cut uses an ideal rectangular section and linear drift of the floor at the cut centerline. The check samples the worst depth along the rear island and does not resolve floor variation across the cut width. A silicon remnant is counted as a candidate bridge only. A cut-through breach or a tangent join is flagged without inventing an electrical connection. Phase, electrode impedance and actual production tolerances are outside the fixture.
