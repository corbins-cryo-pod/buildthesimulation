EVIDENCE LEDGER

What the paper reports

Device and sections
Srinivasan et al. (2015), Biomaterials 41:151–165. See §§2.1–2.2 and 3.1, 3.7, and Figures 2, 3 and 9. Georgia Tech-hosted full article.
Layer order
40 µm lower PDMS, 100 µm tall and 20 µm wide SU-8 walls, 10 µm PDMS cap. The Ti/Au-treated lower glass and the upper PAA release layer are temporary supports with different roles.
Counts and variants
Open grooves precede capping. The capped rolled builds use 143, 84, or 59 patterned lumens at widths of 50, 100, or 150 µm. The nominal axial length after trim is 3 mm; roll diameter is about 1.5 mm. The small open core is not a mask-defined lumen.
Why the wider build is wired
§3.7 names the 150 × 100 µm scaffold, not the 100 × 100 µm scaffold, for the eight 60 µm stainless steel wires. Subtracting a nominal 60 µm disk yields 12,173 µm² of free area there; the comparison in a 100 µm-wide geometry gives 7,173 µm². These are geometric differences, not tissue or usability thresholds.
Real failures vs our test
§3.8 reports nine broken leads among forty implanted wire positions across five devices, leaving thirty-one functioning electrodes in that study. The synthetic diagrams do not simulate aging, tissue response, usable signals or observed clinical yield. The source did not publish the eight exact lumen assignments.
ASSUMPTIONS AND EQUATIONS

What this bench computes

Archimedean representation
Use a teaching core radius of 170 µm and advance 150 µm radially per full turn. Sample the ribbon by equal lengths of the flat pitch along the centerline. Each polygon is a local wedge; caliper spans come from the modeled outline, not a historical roll scan. Display rotation moves the scaffold and stencil together.
Relative widening
Study §3.1 compares the inner two rings with the outermost ring: approximately 25%, 2.5%, 7.5% extra area for the 50, 100, 150 µm branches. A smooth width trend normalized to those average ratios is a schematic interpolation. The actual per-lumen widths and the core are not measured here.
Stencilled registration
Intersect convex polygons between the reconstructed cut face and a virtual duplicate stencil translated in X/Y. Teaching acceptance uses at least 80% overlap with the matching aperture and at most 2% overlap with other apertures. The paper does not name this fixture or these tolerances.
Benign-fluid surrogate
Bruus, Theoretical Microfluidics, §2.4.5, Eq. (2.48d): rectangular pressure-driven flow. Here 41 odd Fourier terms approximate the square/rectangular correction. The local tube, optional wired section, blockage and matched stencil resistance are in series. Replace wire/overlap area losses by aspect-preserving rectangular cross-sections; no leakage, cross-flow, fluid/composite coupling, exact annular resistance, or binding model is inferred. Sums assume independent apertures at the same ΔP.
Data scope
Source geometry and process stages are separately tagged from fixture assumptions. No author masks, batch recipes, qualification limits, process rights, tissue procedures or clinical use. OBJ/MTL are editable schematic meshes in millimetres; interfaces need not match a production part or incorporate all virtual faults.
