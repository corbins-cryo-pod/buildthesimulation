FAULT ISOLATION / SELECTED DISC

What passes this fixture?

A pin connects only to its identically numbered disc, without any other pin or the frame. This is a binary, dry test: absent edges mean open circuits and connections have no modeled resistance. The independent aperture teaching gate requires at least 95% visible face, not a manufacturer limit.

03 / PROVENANCE

What is known, drawn or assumed?

PUBLISHED

Original eight-site configuration

Oxley et al. show an eight-disc example. The nominal bought scaffold is 31.1 mm long when described at 3.0 mm expanded diameter; discs are 750 µm across and 50 µm thick, and the insulated Pt/W conductor core is 25 µm. Strut crossings were reported approximately 2.5 mm apart. This is one documented configuration, not every early Stentrode.

MODEL

One declared lattice

We choose 12 axial intervals, eight angular divisions, eight example crossings and an identity fixture pinout. For mandrel diameter D, each diagonal’s unwrapped circumferential run is πD/8. We preserve its length and recalculate the axial run. Strut widths, elastic properties and the original masked pin layout are not recovered.

DERIVED

Metal face versus aperture

The nominal face is π(0.375 mm)² = 0.4418 mm². A straight mask edge covers a circular segment. At 50% diameter penetration exactly half the face is obscured. Hiding metal in this geometry never opens its welded electrical path.

Why later 16-contact versions are not presets in this lab

SWITCH, 2023: a 16-electrode sensing system links to an implanted receiver/transmitter. The paper reports a 0.3 mm² electrode surface area. Neither the count nor this area belongs to the eight-disc prototype.

Kacker et al., 2025: later participant data describe 16 contacts, including a 500 µm diameter reference. Nominal geometric face area and separately published electrode surface area are not interchangeable measurements. Neither supplies a complete vendor traveler.

Nankai / Rongyuan: a separately developed interventional BCI. Its contacts, materials and manufacturing route are not inferred from the Oxley 2016 reference.

Sources and evidence limits

Geometry OBJ: exports the assembled model at equal scale in all axes. All coordinates are millimetres (scale by 0.001 when importing to metres). Scaffold struts export as zero-width centerlines rather than fabricated sections. The disc envelopes are solids and any selected face obstruction is a marked witness surface.

Virtual manufacturing and inert inspection only. No physical equipment controls or implantation instructions. No frame-force, tissue, neural signal, electrochemistry, clinical or chronic-lifetime simulation. A successful fixture audit is not certification of a medical device.
